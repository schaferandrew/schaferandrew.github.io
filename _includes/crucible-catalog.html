{%- comment -%} The Crucible catalog for Liquid pages: modes, areas, tiers and tasks from the newest results bundle in _data/crucible/, with _data/crucible_fallbacks.yml filling what a format-2 bundle lacks. Include it, then read cr_latest, cr_tasks, cr_modes, cr_tiers and cr_fb.
