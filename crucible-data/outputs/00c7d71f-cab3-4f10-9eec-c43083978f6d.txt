1. To calculate the value of $5,000 after 3 years with monthly compounding interest at 4%, we use the formula for compound interest:
   \[
   A = P (1 + \frac{r}{n})^{nt}
   \]
   where \(P\) is the principal amount ($5,000), \(r\) is the annual interest rate (0.04), \(n\) is the number of times interest is compounded per year (12), and \(t\) is the time the money is invested for in years (3).

   Plugging in the values:
   \[
   A = 5000 \left(1 + \frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{12 \times 3} = 5000 \left(1 + 0.003333\right)^{36}
   \]
   \[
   A = 5000 \times (1.003333)^{36} \approx 5000 \times 1.124978 \approx 5624.89
   \]

   To the nearest cent, it is worth approximately $5624.89.

2. To determine how many boxes of flooring are needed to cover a 1,850 sq ft space with an extra 10% waste, we first calculate the total area that needs to be covered:
   \[
   \text{Total area} = 1850 - (1850 \times 0.1) = 1850 - 185 = 1665 \text{ sq ft}
   \]

   Each box covers 23.5 sq ft, so the number of boxes needed is:
   \[
   \text{Number of boxes} = \frac{1665}{23.5} \approx 70.48
   \]
   Since we can't buy a fraction of a box, we round up to the next whole number:
   \[
   \text{Number of boxes} = 71
   \]

   To the nearest cent, you need to buy 71 boxes.

3. To calculate the cost of gas for a 412-mile trip at $3.89 per gallon and including a 10% tip:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 412 \times 3.89 \times (1 + 0.10) = 412 \times 3.89 \times 1.10
   \]
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 412 \times 4.279 = 1756.828
   \]

   To the nearest cent, the gas costs approximately $1756.83.

4. To calculate the total cost of dinner including tax and splitting it into four ways:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 186.40 + (186.40 \times 0.18) = 186.40 + 33.912 = 210.312
   \]
   \[
   \text{Cost per person} = \frac{210.312}{4} = 52.578
   \]

   To the nearest cent, each person pays approximately $52.58.
