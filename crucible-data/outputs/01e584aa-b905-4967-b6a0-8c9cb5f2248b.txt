**Anachronisms**

c) News of Lee’s defeat at Gettysburg – the battle ended July 3 1863, so no news could reach Ohio on June 10.

f) Cousin in Cleveland who telephoned with the news – the telephone was not invented until 1876; telephones did not exist in 1863.

g) Fencing the north pasture with barbed wire – barbed‑wire fencing was patented in 1874, long after 1863.

j) West Virginia, which joined the Union last month – West Virginia became a state on January 12 1863; it had not joined “last month” (May).

k) The draft riots in New York – those riots erupted July 13‑16 1863, after the June 10 date.

**ANACHRONISMS: c,f,g,j,k**
