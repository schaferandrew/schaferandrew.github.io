Brazil is the only country whose territory is traversed by both the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn, as the equator runs through its northern Amazon region while the tropic passes through its southern states.  
Saudi Arabia uniquely possesses coastline on both the Red Sea to the west and the Persian Gulf to the east.  
Reykjavík, Iceland, lies at roughly 64° N, making it the highest‑latitude capital among all sovereign states.  
France, with its extensive overseas departments and territories, spans twelve time zones, more than any other nation when those territories are counted.  
Maine is the US state whose easternmost point (West Quoddy Head) is closest to mainland Africa across the Atlantic Ocean.  
Lesotho is the sole landlocked sovereign country that shares a border with exactly one other country, namely South Africa.  

**ANSWERS**  
1. Brazil  
2. Saudi Arabia  
3. Reykjavík  
4. France  
5. Maine  
6. Lesotho
