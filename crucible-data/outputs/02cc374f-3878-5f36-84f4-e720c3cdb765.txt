

**Answer: Dave sits in chair 3.**

**Assumptions**
- "Immediately to the left" means the adjacent seat one number lower; chairs 1→4 run left→right.
- "Somewhere to the right" means any seat with a higher number (not necessarily adjacent).

**Reasoning**
1. Dave = red (clue 5); Carol = yellow (clue 4); so the green chair (chair 1, clue 3) is occupied by Alice or Bob.
2. Carol must sit to the right of Dave (red) (clues 4–5).
3. Alice is immediately left of blue (clue 1), so Alice is in chair 1, 2, or 3.
   - **Alice in 2** → chair 3 = blue. Chair 1 must be Bob (only one left who can be green). Then Dave/Carol take chairs 3/4 with Dave left of Carol, forcing Dave = chair 3 = red ≠ blue. Contradiction.
   - **Alice in 3** → chair 4 = blue. Chair 1 must be Bob. Dave/Carol take 2/4; Carol must be right of Dave → Carol in 4, but chair 4 = blue ≠ yellow. Contradiction.
   - **Alice in 1** → Alice = green, chair 2 = blue (Bob, since Dave/Carol are red/yellow; Bob ≠ chair 4 ✓). Dave in 3 (red), Carol in 4 (yellow), Carol right of Dave ✓.
4. Unique solution: 1 = Alice (green), 2 = Bob (blue), 3 = Dave (red), 4 = Carol (yellow).

**Ambiguity note:** If "somewhere to the right" were read as *immediately* right, the only candidate placement (Dave 3, Carol 4) still holds, so the answer is unchanged under either reading.
