5. **Alaska** – The westernmost point of Alaska lies just over 9 000 km from mainland Africa, making it the U.S. state closest to the African continent.  
6. **Lesotho** – Lesotho is a landlocked country that borders only South Africa, and unlike Italy’s microstates (San Marino, Vatican City), it is not a microstate itself.
