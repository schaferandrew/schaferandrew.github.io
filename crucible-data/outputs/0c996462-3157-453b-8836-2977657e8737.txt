c) News of Lee's defeat at Gettysburg – Gettysburg occurred on July 2–3, 1863, so news would not have reached Ohio until after that date.  
d) The telegraph bringing news from Washington – Telegraphs were operational and widely used during the Civil War to transmit military updates.  
e) The Monitor's fight with the Merrimack last year – This battle occurred on March 9, 1862; it was reported in June 1863.  
f) A cousin in Cleveland who telephoned with the news – Telephones were not invented until 1876; no telephone could exist in 1863.  
g) Fencing the north pasture with barbed wire – Barbed wire was patented in 1874 and not used for fencing until after that date.  
h) Mathew Brady's photographs of the dead at Antietam – The battle occurred on September 17, 1862; photos were taken then and reported later.  
i) Stonewall Jackson's death last month – He died in May 1863; his death was known by June.  
j) West Virginia, which joined the Union last month – West Virginia declared independence on June 1, 1861; it had been a state since then.
