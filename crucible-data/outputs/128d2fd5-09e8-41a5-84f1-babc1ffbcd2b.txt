**1. Savings account**

Principal = \$5 000  
Annual rate = 4 % → monthly rate \(r = \dfrac{0.04}{12}=0.003333\overline{3}\)  

Number of months in 3 years: \(n = 3\times12 = 36\)

Future value formula (compound interest)  

\[
A = P(1+r)^{n}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
A &= 5000\bigl(1+0.003333\overline{3}\bigr)^{36} \\
  &= 5000\,(1.003333\overline{3})^{36} \\
  &\approx 5000 \times 1.1272988 \\
  &\approx 5636.494
\end{aligned}
\]

Rounded to the nearest cent: **\$5 636.49**

**2. Flooring a room**

Room area = 1 850 ft²  
Boxes supply 23.5 ft² each, need 10 % extra for waste.

\[
\text{Total required} = 1\,850 \times 1.10 = 2\,035\ \text{ft}^2
\]

Number of boxes needed  

\[
\frac{2\,035}{23.5}=86.79\;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; \lceil 86.79\rceil = 87
\]

So **87 boxes** must be bought.

**3. Gas cost for the road trip**

Distance = 412 mi  
Fuel efficiency = 34 mpg → gallons needed  

\[
\text{gallons}= \frac{412}{34}=12.117647\ldots
\]

Gas price = \$3.89 per gallon  

\[
\text{Cost}=12.117647 \times 3.89 \approx 47.1376
\]

Rounded to the nearest cent: **\$47.14**

**4. Dinner and tip split**

Pre‑tax amount = \$172.00  
Tax already included → total after tax = \$186.40 (matches 172 + tax).  

Tip = 18 % of pre‑tax amount  

\[
\text{Tip}=0.18 \times 172 = 30.96? \; \text{Wait, recalc: } 0.1\times172=17.2,\;0.05\times172=8.6\;\Rightarrow\;25.8
\]

So tip = \$25.80.

Total with tip  

\[
186.40 + 25.80 = 212.20
\]

Split four ways  

\[
\frac{212.20}{4}=53.05
\]

Each person pays **\$53.05**.
