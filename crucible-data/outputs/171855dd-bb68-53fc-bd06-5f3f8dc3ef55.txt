These are mistakes that must be corrected to ensure the email is grammatically correct and professional.

*   **Subject-Verb Agreement:** The phrase *"one of the two items were damaged"* contains an error. The subject is "one" (singular), so the verb must be singular.
    *   *Correction:* "...one of the two items **was** damaged."
*   **Spelling/Apostrophe:** The word *"havent"* is missing an apostrophe.
    *   *Correction:* "**haven't**".
*   **Word Form:** The phrase *"box was clearly crush"* uses a verb/noun where a past participle/adjective is needed.
    *   *Correction:* "...box was clearly **crushed**..."
*   **Punctuation:** There is a missing comma before the coordinating conjunction "but" in the sentence *"I contacted support last week but I havent heard anything back."* When joining two independent clauses, a comma is required before "but."
