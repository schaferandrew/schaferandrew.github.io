## Reasoning

**Objective language errors (spelling, grammar, agreement, word choice):**

- **3.** Subject–verb agreement: the subject of "is" is *prices* (plural), so it must be "**are**."
- **6.** "it's" is a contraction of "it is"; the possessive form needed here is "**its**."
- **12.** Comparative conjunction: you need "**than**," not "then."

**Claims/wording that are overstated, imprecise, unclear, or misleading:**

- **1.** Presents monetary expansion as *the* cause of inflation. Inflation can also come from demand-pull and cost-push factors, so this is an incomplete (imprecise) definition.
- **4.** False: the Fed has no legal mandate to raise rates once CPI exceeds 2%; that figure is a stated goal, not a statutory trigger.
- **8.** Calling people unemployed "lazy" mischaracterizes cyclical/structural unemployment; it's misleading.
- **11.** Overstates both certainty and consequence—QE isn't simply "printing money," and it does not *inevitably* produce hyperinflation.
- **14.** One episode (Japan) doesn't *prove* a universal conclusion, and "completely ineffective" overstates the case; monetary policy has had real effects elsewhere.
- **18.** Overstated: supply-side economics remains contested rather than "definitively debunked," and the economy did grow in the 1980s despite larger deficits ("without growing the economy at all" is false).

The remaining sentences are grammatically sound and factually defensible, so they get no flag.
