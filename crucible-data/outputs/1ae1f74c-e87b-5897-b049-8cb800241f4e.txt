1. To find the value of the savings account, we use the compound interest formula: $A = P(1 + \frac{r}{n})^{nt}$
   $P = 5,000$
   $r = 0.04$
   $n = 12$ (compounded monthly)
   $t = 3$
   $A = 5,000 \times (1 + \frac{0.04}{12})^{(12 \times 3)}$
   $A = 5,000 \times (1.003333333333...)^{36}$
   $A = 5,000 \times 1.1272718793...$
   $A = 5,636.35939...$
   Rounded to the cent: $5,636.36

2. First, calculate the total square footage needed including the 10% waste:

   $2,035 / 23.5 = 86.59574468...$
   Since you cannot purchase a fraction of a box, you must round up to the next whole box:
   87 boxes

3. First, calculate the total gallons of gas needed:

4. First, calculate the tip based on the pre-tax amount:
