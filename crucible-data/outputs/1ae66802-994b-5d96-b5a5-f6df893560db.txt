

## Objective errors

These are clear grammar, spelling, or punctuation errors:

1. **“one of the two items were damaged” → “one of the two items was damaged”**  
   - The subject is **“one”**, which is singular, so the verb should be **“was”**, not **“were”**.

2. **“havent” → “haven’t”**  
   - Missing apostrophe in the contraction.

3. **“The box was clearly crush” → “The box was clearly crushed”**  
   - The past participle **“crushed”** is needed here.

---

## Optional style improvements

These are not required errors, but they would improve clarity, flow, or formatting:

- Add a comma before **“but”**:  
  **“I contacted support last week, but I haven’t heard anything back.”**  
  This helps separate two complete clauses.

- Use an en dash in the range:  
  **“5–7 business days”** instead of **“5-7 business days”**.

- Capitalize **“Order”** in the subject line:  
  **“Refund for Order 84721”**

- For clarity, consider replacing:  
  **“which seem to have passed already for my earlier request”**  
  with something like:  
  **“and that timeframe seems to have already passed for my earlier request.”**

- **“Can you tell me what the status is?”** is acceptable, but **“Can you tell me the current status?”** is slightly smoother.

## Corrected version

Subject: Refund for order 84721

Hi Support,

I am writing because my order arrived three days late, and one of the two items was damaged. I contacted support last week but I haven’t heard anything back. The box was clearly crushed on one side, and the mug inside has a crack through the handle. I would like a refund for the mug, but please let me know if you need pictures of it. Also, your website says refunds are processed in 5-7 business days, which seem to have passed already for my earlier request. Can you tell me what the status is? Thanks for your help.
