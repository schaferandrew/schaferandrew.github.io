Here's my fact-checking of each claim, with brief reasoning:

**Accurate claims:**
1. **Battle of Hastings (1066)** — Correct. William the Conqueror defeated Harold Godwinson on 14 October 1066.
4. **Declaration of Independence adopted July 4, 1776** — Correct. It was adopted/signed that date (though some delegates signed later).
5. **Storming of the Bastille in July 1789** — Correct. This happened on 14 July 1789.
8. **India's independence in August 1947** — Correct. Independence came on 15 August 1947.
9. **Armstrong and Aldrin landed July 1969** — Correct. Apollo 11 landed on 20–21 July 1969.
11. **Germany reunified the year after the Wall fell** — Correct. The Wall fell in November 1989; reunification occurred on 3 October 1990.
12. **Mandela elected President of South Africa in 1994** — Correct. He won the first multiracial election and took office in May 1994.

**Inaccurate claims:**
2. Magna Carta was sealed by **King John**, not King Richard, in 1215.
3. Columbus first reached the Americas in **1492**, not 1493.
6. Waterloo took place in **1815**, not 1812 (the year of the Russian campaign).
7. The Titanic sank on 15 April **1912**, not 1913.
10. The Berlin Wall fell in November **1989**, not 1991.
