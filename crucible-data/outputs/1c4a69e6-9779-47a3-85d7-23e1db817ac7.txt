1. Brazil is the only country in the world traversed by both the Equator across its northern region and the Tropic of Capricorn across its southern region.
2. Saudi Arabia is the sole nation on the Arabian Peninsula with coastlines on both the Red Sea to the west and the Persian Gulf to the east.
3. Reykjavík, Iceland sits at roughly 64° N, farther north than any other capital belonging to a sovereign state.
4. Including overseas territories from the Caribbean to the Pacific, France spans twelve time zones—more than Russia—making it the country with the widest time zone spread.
5. The curvature of the Earth makes the great-circle route from Maine’s easternmost point to Morocco shorter than any path to mainland Africa from Florida.
6. Lesotho is an enclave completely surrounded by South Africa, making it the only landlocked sovereign state outside the Italian microstates that borders exactly one other country.
