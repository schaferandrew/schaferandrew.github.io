

a) The Emancipation Proclamation took effect on January 1, 1863, so by June 10, it was already in effect. Referring to it "taking effect this New Year's Day" (January 1, 1864) is incorrect.  
c) The Battle of Gettysburg occurred July 1–3, 1863. On June 10, the defeat had not yet happened.  
f) The telephone was invented in 1876; no telephones existed in 1863.  
g) Barbed wire was patented in 1874; fencing with it in 1863 is anachronistic.  
j) West Virginia joined the Union on June 20, 1863. Referring to it as joining "last month" (May) is incorrect.  
k) The New York Draft Riots occurred in July 1863, after June 10.
