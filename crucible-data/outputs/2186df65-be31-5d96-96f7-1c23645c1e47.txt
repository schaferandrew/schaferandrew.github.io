***Briefing for Tuesday, March 10***

### Local Politics and Finance
**Transit Levy Approved (Ardmore Ledger, Lakeshore Public Radio)**
The Port Ardmore City Council voted 7-2 late Monday to approve a $41.6 million transit levy, which will be spread over ten years. The funds are designated for three new bus routes and the rebuild of the Fulton Street station. Council member Ines Varga, who voted no, stated that the city had not published a cost estimate for households. According to Lakeshore Public Radio (8:20 a.m.), Mayor Theo Brandt called the levy "the biggest investment in buses this city has made in a generation," and collection would begin July 1 if the levy passes its second reading on March 24.

**Rent Stabilization Rally (Ardmore Ledger, Lakeshore Public Radio)**
Supporters of a proposed rent stabilization ordinance rallied outside City Hall Monday evening. The Ardmore Ledger estimated the crowd at about 300, while organizers told Lakeshore Public Radio that more than 1,000 people attended. Police estimated the crowd at 400.

**Teacher Pay Bill Advances (Statewide Wire)**
The state legislature passed a bill raising the minimum teacher salary to $48,500. The vote counts were 39-28 in the Senate and 81-52 in the House. A spokesperson noted that Governor Lena Ruiz is expected to sign it this week, and the increase would take effect in the fall term.

**Opinion on Levy (The Ardmore Bugle)**
In a column published in The Ardmore Bugle, Rob Haddad argued that the transit levy is a "tax grab," estimating it would cost a typical household $900 a year and criticizing the council for rushing the vote.

### Local Safety Incident
**Kettle Street Fire (Statewide Wire, Lakeshore Public Radio)**
A fire broke out early Tuesday at a vacant warehouse on Kettle Street in Port Ardmore. Initially, Statewide Wire reported that two firefighters were injured. However, a later update from Lakeshore Public Radio (11:40 a.m.) stated that Fire Chief Ama Mensah clarified that only one firefighter was treated for smoke inhalation and released, noting the earlier reports were incorrect. Crews had brought the fire under control by 9:15 a.m., and the cause remains under investigation.

### International News
**Veloria Interest Rate Hike (Global Dispatch)**
The central bank of Veloria raised its benchmark interest rate by 0.25 percentage points to 3.75%. This marks the third increase this year, citing inflation of 4.1%.
