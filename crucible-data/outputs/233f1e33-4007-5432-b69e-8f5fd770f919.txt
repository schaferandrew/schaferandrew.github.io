**Port Ardmore News Briefing: Tuesday, March 10**

**Transit Levy Approved by City Council**
The Port Ardmore City Council voted 7-2 late Monday to approve a $41.6 million transit levy spread over ten years. According to the *Ardmore Ledger*, the funds will be used to rebuild the Fulton Street station and fund three new bus routes. Council member Ines Varga, who voted no, stated the city has not published a cost estimate for households. The *Ardmore Ledger* reports the levy requires a second reading on March 24.

Mayor Theo Brandt called the levy "the biggest investment in buses this city has made in a generation," according to *Lakeshore Public Radio*. *Lakeshore Public Radio* also reported that collection would begin July 1 if the levy passes its second reading.

**Kettle Street Warehouse Fire**
A fire broke out early Tuesday at a vacant warehouse on Kettle Street in Port Ardmore. The *Statewide Wire* initially reported that two firefighters were injured. However, *Lakeshore Public Radio* later reported that Fire Chief Ama Mensah corrected this, stating one firefighter was treated for smoke inhalation and released. *Lakeshore Public Radio* also reported that crews had the fire under control by 9:15 a.m. The cause is under investigation.

**Rent Stabilization Rally**
Supporters of a proposed rent stabilization ordinance rallied outside City Hall on Monday evening. Reports on the size of the crowd vary:
*   *Ardmore Ledger* reporters estimated the crowd at about 300.
*   *Lakeshore Public Radio* reported that organizers said more than 1,000 people attended, while police estimated the crowd at 400.

**State Teacher Pay Bill**
The state legislature passed a bill to raise the minimum teacher salary to $48,500, with Senate votes of 39-28 and House votes of 81-52, according to the *Statewide Wire*. A spokesperson said Governor Lena Ruiz is expected to sign the bill this week, which would take effect in the fall term.

**International News**
The central bank of Veloria raised its benchmark interest rate by 0.25 percentage points to 3.75%, marking its third increase this year due to 4.1% inflation, according to *Global Dispatch*.
