## Working

**1. Compound interest (monthly)**

$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt} = 5000\left(1 + \frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{12 \times 3} = 5000(1.003333...)^{36}$$

$(1.003333...)^{36} \approx 1.12727187$

$$A = 5000 \times 1.12727187 = 5636.3594\ldots \rightarrow \$5{,}636.36$$

**2. Boxes of flooring**

Total area needed (with waste): $1850 \times 1.10 = 2035$ sq ft

Boxes required: $\dfrac{2035}{23.5} = 86.5957\ldots$

You can't buy a partial box, so round **up** to the next whole box → **87 boxes**.
(Check: $87 \times 23.5 = 2044.5$ sq ft ≥ 2035 ✓)

**3. Gas cost**

$$\text{Cost} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{mpg}} \times \text{price} = \frac{412}{34} \times 3.89 = 12.117647... \times 3.89 = 47.1376\ldots \rightarrow \$47.14$$

**4. Dinner split four ways**

Tip (on pre-tax amount): $172.00 \times 0.18 = 30.96$

Total to split: $186.40 + 30.96 = 217.36$

Per person: $\dfrac{217.36}{4} = 54.34 \rightarrow \$54.34$
