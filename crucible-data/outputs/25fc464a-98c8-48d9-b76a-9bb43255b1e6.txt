The signing of Magna Carta (1215) is the earliest event, followed by the printing of the Gutenberg Bible (c. 1450), then the start of the First World War (1914). The French Revolution began in 1789, which comes before the fall of the Berlin Wall in 1989 and the first Moon landing in 1969.

The first Moon landing — 1969
