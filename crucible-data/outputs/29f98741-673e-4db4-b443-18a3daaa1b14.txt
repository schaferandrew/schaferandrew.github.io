### Problem 1

    The exponent of the group is $\text{lcm}(2, 32) = 32$.

    $\gcd(12, 54) = 2 \cdot 3 = 6$.
    $S_2 = 6$.

3.  **Modulo $7^2 = 49$**:
    $(\mathbb{Z}/49\mathbb{Z})^\times$ is cyclic of order $\phi(49) = 49(1-1/7) = 42$.
    Number of solutions is $\gcd(12, 42)$.
    $42 = 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 7$, $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$.
    $\gcd(12, 42) = 2 \cdot 3 = 6$.
    $S_3 = 6$.

4.  **Modulo $13^1 = 13$**:
    $(\mathbb{Z}/13\mathbb{Z})^\times$ is cyclic of order $\phi(13) = 12$.
    Number of solutions is $\gcd(12, 12) = 12$.
    $S_4 = 12$.

Total solutions $S = S_1 S_2 S_3 S_4 = 8 \times 6 \times 6 \times 12$.
$8 \times 6 = 48$.
$6 \times 12 = 72$.
$48 \times 72 = 48(70 + 2) = 3360 + 96 = 3456$.

**Answer:** 3456
