The Port Ardmore City Council approved a $41.6 million transit levy on a 7‑2 vote, with Mayor Theo Brandt calling it “the biggest investment in buses this city has made in a generation.” The levy will fund three new bus routes and the rebuild of Fulton Street station; collection would begin July 1 if the measure passes its second reading on March 24. Ines Varga opposed the levy, citing the lack of a published cost estimate for households. [S1, S2]  

A fire erupted early Tuesday at a vacant warehouse on Kettle Street. Initial reports said two firefighters were injured, but Fire Chief Ama Mensah later clarified that only one firefighter was treated for smoke inhalation and released; crews had the blaze under control by 9:15 a.m., and the cause remains under investigation. [S3, S4]  

Supporters of a proposed rent‑stabilization ordinance rallied outside City Hall on Monday evening. Reporters at the scene estimated about 300 attendees, while organizers claimed more than 1,000 people were present; police estimated the crowd at 400. [S5, S6]  

The state legislature passed a bill raising the minimum teacher salary to $48,500 by votes of 39‑28 in the Senate and 81‑52 in the House, with Governor Lena Ruiz expected to sign it this week; the increase would take effect in the fall term. [S7]  

The central bank of Veloria raised its benchmark interest rate by 0.25 percentage points to 3.75%, citing an inflation rate of 4.1%. [S8]
