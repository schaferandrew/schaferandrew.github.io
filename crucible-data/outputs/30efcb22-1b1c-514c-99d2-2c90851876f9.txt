**Features**
- Inputs: `-p` principal, `-r` annual rate (percent), `-t` time in years (`5y`, `5 years`, bare `5`) **and** months (`18m`, `18 months`), `-n` compounding frequency (integer or `annually`/`semiannually`/`quarterly`/`monthly`/`weekly`/`daily`), optional `-c` recurring contribution with `-f` contribution frequency
- Readable summary prints principal, rate, time, compounding, recurring contribution, total contributions, interest earned, and final balance
- Contributions earn interest from deposit date via equivalent periodic rate `j = (1+r/n)^(n/f) − 1`; zero-rate and fractional-period cases handled
- Validation (exit 1 with `error: ...` on stderr) for: non-numeric/non-finite values, negative principal/rate/time/contribution, zero time, non-integer or sub-1 frequencies, and the contradictory zero-principal + zero-contribution case

**Tests cover** ordinary annual case, zero interest, monthly compounding, recurring contributions, known expected value ($1,000 @ 5% monthly × 1 yr = $1,051.161898), plus parsing/validation/CLI error paths.
