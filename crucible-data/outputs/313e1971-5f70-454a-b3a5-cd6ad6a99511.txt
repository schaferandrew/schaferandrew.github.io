Here's my reasoning for dating each event:

- **Magna Carta** was signed by King John of England in 1215, establishing the principle that everyone, including the monarch, was subject to the law.
- **Gutenberg Bible** was printed by Johannes Gutenberg around 1455, one of the first major books printed using movable type in Europe.
- **French Revolution** began in 1789 with events like the storming of the Bastille on July 14.
- **First World War** started in 1914 following the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand and subsequent declarations of war.
- **First Moon landing** occurred in 1969 when Apollo 11 astronauts landed on the Moon.
- **Fall of the Berlin Wall** happened in 1989, marking a pivotal moment in the end of the Cold War.

ORDER:
1. The signing of Magna Carta — 1215
2. The printing of the Gutenberg Bible — c. 1455
3. The start of the French Revolution — 1789
4. The start of the First World War — 1914
5. The first Moon landing — 1969
6. The fall of the Berlin Wall — 1989
