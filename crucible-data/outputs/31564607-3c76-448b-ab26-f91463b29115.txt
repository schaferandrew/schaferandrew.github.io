# Analysis of Each Item (Letter dated June 10, 1863)

**a) Emancipation Proclamation taking effect this New Year's Day** — NOT anachronism. It was issued/effective January 1, 1863, so it had already happened by June 10. ✓

**b) Siege of Vicksburg dragging on** — NOT anachronism. The siege ran May 18 – July 4, 1863, so it was indeed ongoing in early June. ✓

**c) News of Lee's defeat at Gettysburg** — **ANACHRONISM.** Gettysburg ended July 3–4, 1863, nearly a month after this letter's date. ✗

**d) The telegraph bringing news from Washington** — NOT anachronism. The telegraph was in widespread use during the war. ✓

**e) The Monitor's fight with the Merrimack last year** — NOT anachronism. That clash (USS *Monitor* vs. CSS *Virginia*) occurred March 1862, correctly "last year." ✓

**f) A cousin who telephoned with the news** — **ANACHRONISM.** The telephone wasn't invented until 1876. ✗

**g) Fencing the north pasture with barbed wire** — **ANACHRONISM.** Barbed wire was patented in 1874, not available to a farmer in 1863. ✗

**h) Mathew Brady's photographs of the dead at Antietam** — NOT anachronism. Antietam was fought September 17, 1862; Brady photographed the aftermath afterward, so this was known by June 1863. ✓

**i) Stonewall Jackson's death last month** — NOT anachronism. Jackson died May 10, 1863, correctly "last month." ✓

**j) West Virginia, which joined the Union last month** — **ANACHRONISM.** West Virginia was admitted June 20, 1863—ten days *after* this letter's date. ✗

**k) The draft riots in New York** — **ANACHRONISM.** Those riots occurred July 13–16, 1863, not yet a month away. ✗

---

ANACHRONISMS: c, f, g, j, k
