**Objective errors - grammar / spelling / punctuation**

1. **Subject-verb agreement:** *one of the two items were damaged* → *one of the two items was damaged*. "One" is singular.
2. **Missing apostrophe:** *I havent heard* → *I haven't heard*.
3. **Wrong verb form:** *The box was clearly crush on one side* → *The box was clearly crushed on one side*. Past participle needed after "was".
4. **Subject-verb agreement:** *which seem to have passed* → *which seems to have passed*. The relative pronoun refers to the singular time frame "5-7 business days".

**Optional style improvements - not errors**

* Line break / paragraphing after the greeting for readability: `Hi Support,` on its own line.
* Salutation formality: *Hi Support,* is acceptable; *Hello Support Team,* or *Dear Support Team,* is a more conventional business style.
* Comma before coordinating conjunction: *last week but I haven't* could be *last week, but I haven't* for clarity. This is stylistic.
* Consistency of contraction: *I am writing* vs *I'm writing* is a tone choice.
