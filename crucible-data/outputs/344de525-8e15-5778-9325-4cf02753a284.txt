## Root Cause Analysis & Fix Plan

The issue lies in a disconnect between the legal/design requirement for *contributor terms acceptance* and the application's internal readiness gating, specifically tied to the `onboardingComplete` flag.

1.  **Legal/Design Requirement:** An invited contributor gains full standing to contribute once they accept the terms (`termsAcceptedAt` is set).
2.  **Current Implementation Flaw:** The system appears to incorrectly conflate "terms accepted" with needing a fully completed profile (`onboardingComplete: true`) to enter the editor or stabilize the view, causing the reported flashing/blocking. The system might be implicitly requiring a full onboarding experience even when contributor terms are met.

**Fix:**
The application must recognize that once `termsAcceptedAt` is set for an account, the contributor has met the minimal requirement to start contributing/using the editor. Any middleware or service layer logic that checks for `onboardingComplete` should be relaxed or bypassed when the account status is transitioning from contributor setup to active use.
