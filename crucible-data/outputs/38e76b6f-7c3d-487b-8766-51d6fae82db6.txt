1. While many assume an African or Asian nation, Brazil is the only sovereign country whose mainland is intersected by both lines of latitude.
2. Although Egypt and Jordan are frequently guessed due to the Suez Canal and Gulf of Aqaba, only Saudi Arabia possesses direct maritime borders on both bodies of water.
3. While Moscow or Helsinki are often mistakenly cited due to their high latitudes, Reykjavik sits at approximately 64°N, making it the true northernmost capital of an independent nation.
4. Though Russia and the United States are commonly assumed to hold the record, France’s widely dispersed overseas departments and territories actually give it 12 (or 13) time zones, surpassing them all.
5. While Florida is the intuitive choice, the curvature of the Earth and specific longitudinal alignment mean the great-circle distance from Dakar to the Maine coast is actually shorter.
6. Although Portugal and Ireland each border only one country, they are not landlocked, leaving Lesotho as the sole landlocked nation entirely surrounded by a single neighbor.

ANSWERS
1. Brazil
2. Saudi Arabia
3. Reykjavik, Iceland
4. France
5. Maine
6. Lesotho
