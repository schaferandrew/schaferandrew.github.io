# Geography Questions

**1. Which country is crossed by both the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn?**

Brazil is the only country whose territory stretches from roughly 5°N (north of the Equator, through the Amazon) to about 33°S (south of the Tropic of Capricorn, in the south), so both lines cross its landmass.

**2. Which country has coastline on both the Red Sea and the Persian Gulf?**

Saudi Arabia's western coast runs along the Red Sea while its eastern coast runs along the Persian Gulf, making it the sole country with coastline on both bodies of water.

**3. What is the northernmost capital city of a sovereign state?**

Reykjavik, Iceland (≈ 64.15° N) holds the title; the slightly more northerly Nuuk belongs to Greenland, which is an autonomous territory of Denmark rather than a sovereign state.

**4. Counting overseas territories, which country spans the most time zones?**

France spans 12 time zones (UTC −10 in French Polynesia through UTC +12 in Wallis and Futuna), edging out Russia's 11, because its scattered overseas collectivities in the Pacific, Indian Ocean, and Atlantic add zones that Russia's contiguous territory does not.

**5. Which US state is closest to mainland Africa?**

The Florida Keys (southern tip of Florida) are the nearest point in the United States to the western bulge of mainland Africa (Senegal/Mauritania), roughly 5,000 km by great-circle route.

**6. Apart from the microstates inside Italy, which is the only landlocked country that borders exactly one other country?**

Lesotho is a complete enclave surrounded by South Africa, making it the only landlocked nation other than San Marino and Vatican City that shares a border with just one neighbour.

## ANSWERS

1. Brazil
2. Saudi Arabia
3. Reykjavik, Iceland
4. France
5. Florida
6. Lesotho
