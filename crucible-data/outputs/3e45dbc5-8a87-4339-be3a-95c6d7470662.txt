1. **Brazil** is the only country crossed by both lines: the Equator passes through its north near Macapá, and the Tropic of Capricorn passes through its south near São Paulo. Indonesia's southern edge stops far short of the Tropic.

2. **Saudi Arabia** is the only country with coastline on both the Red Sea (west) and the Persian Gulf (east). Egypt and Iran each touch only one of them.

3. **Reykjavik** (Iceland, about 64°N) is the northernmost capital of a sovereign state, farther north than Oslo or Helsinki. Nuuk is farther north still but is the capital of Greenland, which is not sovereign.

4. **France** spans the most time zones, about 12 (13 if its Antarctic claim is counted), because of overseas territories such as French Polynesia, Réunion, New Caledonia and French Guiana. Russia has 11 on its own landmass, and the US has about 11 with its territories.

5. **Maine** is the US state closest to mainland Africa. The Atlantic bulge of Morocco lies about 5,100 km from Maine's coast, closer than to Florida or Massachusetts, because the great-circle route runs northwest and Maine sits farthest northeast.

6. **Lesotho** is entirely surrounded by South Africa, which makes it the only landlocked country with a single neighbour other than San Marino and the Vatican City, which are both enclaved by Italy.

ANSWERS
1. Brazil
2. Saudi Arabia
3. Reykjavik
4. France
5. Maine
6. Lesotho
