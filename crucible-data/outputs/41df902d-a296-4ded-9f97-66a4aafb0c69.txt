1. To calculate the amount of flour for 10 people using the original recipe:
   \[
   \text{Flour for 10 people} = \frac{300 \text{ g}}{4} \times 10 = 750 \text{ g}
   \]
   
2. To calculate the sale price of a jacket after applying a 15% discount:
   - First, find the original price of the jacket: $84.60
   - Calculate the discount amount: 
     \[
     \text{Discount} = 84.60 \times 0.15 = 12.79
     \]
   
   - Subtract the discount from the original price to find the sale price:
     \[
     \text{Sale Price} = 84.60 - 12.79 = 71.81
     \]

**ANSWERS:**
1. **300 g for 10 people**
   \[
   \boxed{750 \text{ g}}
   \]
   
2. **$71.81 to the cent**
