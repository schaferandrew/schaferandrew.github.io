To select names for this character, I focused on the intersection of her age (born circa 1990) and her socioeconomic background. For a working-class Polish-American family in the Upper Midwest, names in the late 80s and early 90s often leaned toward classic, sturdy, or slightly traditional choices that felt grounded rather than trendy. 

To reflect her intelligence and guarded nature, I avoided overly "bubbly" or diminutive names. Instead, I looked for names with sharp consonants or a certain rhythmic weight that suggests competence and a dry wit. The surnames are chosen to be authentically Polish but common enough in the Midwest that they don't feel like a caricature of her heritage.
