### Analysis of Errors

**Objective Errors (Grammar, Spelling, Punctuation)**
*   **Subject-Verb Agreement:** "...one of the two items **were** damaged" should be "...one of the two items **was** damaged" (the subject is "one").
*   **Punctuation:** "**havent**" is missing an apostrophe and should be "**haven't**."
*   **Verb Tense/Form:** "The box was clearly **crush**" should be "The box was clearly **crushed**."

**Optional Style Improvements**
*   **Punctuation:** A comma should be placed before the coordinating conjunction "**but**" in the sentence "I contacted support last week, but I haven't heard anything back" to separate two independent clauses.
*   **Subject-Verb Agreement/Clarity:** "...which **seem** to have passed..." is technically acceptable if referring to the "days," but "**seems**" is often preferred if referring to the timeframe or the situation as a whole.
