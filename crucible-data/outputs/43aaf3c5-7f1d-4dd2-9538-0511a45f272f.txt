## Design Highlights

- **Discrete period simulation** — For annual (p=1), quarterly (p=4), and monthly (p=12) compounding, interest is credited only at actual period ends, exactly as the spec describes. Daily compounding uses a continuous-fractional approximation within months.
- **Contribution order** — At any coincident moment, interest is credited first, then contribution is added.
- **Validation** — All flag constraints are enforced with clear error messages (no tracebacks).
- **40 tests** — Cover ordinary cases, zero interest, all four compounding types, both contribution frequencies and their combinations with different compounding frequencies, JSON output, summary output, known expected-value cases, and every invalid-input scenario from the spec.
