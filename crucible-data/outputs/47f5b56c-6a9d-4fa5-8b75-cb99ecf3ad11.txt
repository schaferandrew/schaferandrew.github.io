**3. Token on a graph (answer 40).** This is undirected vertex geography. The first player wins from v exactly when v lies in every maximum matching. The graph has a matching of size 5, for example 6-10, 3-9, 2-4, 5-11, 1-7, so the maximum is 5, which leaves one vertex unmatched. The vertices that some maximum matching can leave unmatched are exactly those whose removal leaves a perfect matching: 5, 6, 7 and 8. The winning starting vertices are therefore 1, 2, 3, 4, 9, 10 and 11, whose sum is 40.
