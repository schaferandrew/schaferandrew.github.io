**1.** Count multiples in 1..10,000: 3333 are divisible by 3, 1428 by 7, and 476 by 21 (10000/21 = 476.19). By inclusion–exclusion, the number divisible by 3 or 7 is 3333 + 1428 − 476 = 4285. Removing the 476 multiples of 21 leaves 4285 − 476 = 3809.

**2.** Since 7² = 49 and 7⁴ = 2401 ≡ 1 (mod 100), the powers of 7 repeat with period 4. As 2026 ≡ 2 (mod 4), 7^2026 ≡ 7² = 49 (mod 100).

**3.** Let a_n be the number of tilings of a 2×n board. The last column is covered either by a vertical domino (leaving a_{n−1}), or the last two columns are covered by two horizontal dominoes or one 2×2 square (2 ways, leaving a_{n−2}). So a_n = a_{n−1} + 2a_{n−2}, with a_0 = a_1 = 1. This gives 1, 1, 3, 5, 11, 21, 43, 85, 171, 341, 683, 1365, 2731. Check with the closed form (2^{13}+1)/3 = 8193/3 = 2731.

**4.** The probability that the maximum is at most 4 is (4/6)³, and at most 3 is (3/6)³. The probability the maximum is exactly 4 is (64 − 27)/216 = 37/216.

**5.** There are 14 equally likely (sex, day) outcomes per child, so 196 equally likely ordered pairs. Let B be the event that at least one child is a boy born on Tuesday.
- Pairs where child 1 is a Tuesday boy: 14. Pairs where child 2 is: 14. Both: 1. So |B| = 27.
- Within B, both children are boys when the other child is any boy: child 1 is a Tuesday boy and child 2 is a boy (7 options), or child 2 is a Tuesday boy and child 1 is a boy (7 options), minus the overlap of 1. That is 7 + 7 − 1 = 13.
- The probability is 13/27, not 1/2 or 1/3.

ANSWERS
1. 3809
2. 49
3. 2731
4. 37/216
5. 13/27
