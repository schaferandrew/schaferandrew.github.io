1. **Savings Account Calculation**
*   Principal ($P$): $5,000
*   Annual interest rate ($r$): 4% or 0.04
*   Compounding periods per year ($n$): 12
*   Time in years ($t$): 3
*   Formula: $A = P(1 + r/n)^{nt}$
*   $A = 5000(1 + 0.04/12)^{12 \times 3}$
*   $A = 5000(1 + 0.0033333333...)^{36}$
*   $A = 5000(1.0033333333...)^{36}$
*   $A = 5000(1.127270437...)$
*   $A = 5636.352185...$
*   Rounded to the cent: $5,636.35

2. **Flooring Calculation**
*   Area to cover: 1,850 sq ft
*   Waste factor: 10%
*   Total area needed: $1,850 \times 1.10 = 2,035$ sq ft
*   Square footage per box: 23.5 sq ft
*   Number of boxes: $2,035 / 23.5 = 86.59574468...$
*   Since you cannot buy a partial box, you must round up to the nearest whole box.
*   Total boxes: 87

3. **Gas Cost Calculation**
*   Total distance: 412 miles
*   Fuel efficiency: 34 miles per gallon
*   Gallons needed: $412 / 34 = 12.1176470588...$
*   Price per gallon: $3.89
*   Total cost: $12.1176470588... \times 3.89 = 47.1470588235...$
*   Rounded to the cent: $47.15

4. **Dinner Split Calculation**
*   Total including tax: $186.40
*   Pre-tax amount: $172.00
*   Tip amount (18% of pre-tax): $172.00 \times 0.18 = 30.96$
*   Total bill (including tax and tip): $186.40 + 30.96 = 217.36$
*   Split four ways: $217.36 / 4 = 54.34$
*   Amount per person: $54.34

ANSWERS
1. $5,636.35
2. 87
3. $47.15
4. $54.34
