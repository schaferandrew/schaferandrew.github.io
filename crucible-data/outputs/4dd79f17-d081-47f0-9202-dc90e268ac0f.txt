The events span nearly eight centuries. Magna Carta is medieval, Gutenberg's Bible is early print era, the French Revolution is late 18th century, World War I is early 20th century, the Moon landing is late 1960s, and the fall of the Berlin Wall is late 1980s. Ordering them by their commonly cited years gives the sequence from 1215 to 1989.

ORDER:
1. The signing of Magna Carta — 1215
2. The printing of the Gutenberg Bible — 1455
3. The start of the French Revolution — 1789
4. The start of the First World War — 1914
5. The first Moon landing — 1969
6. The fall of the Berlin Wall — 1989
