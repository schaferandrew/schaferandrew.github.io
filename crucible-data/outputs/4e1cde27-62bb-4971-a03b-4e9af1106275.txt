### 1. Compound Interest

**Working:**
*   **Principal ($P$):** $5,000
*   **Annual Interest Rate ($r$):** 4% = 0.04
*   **Compounding Frequency ($n$):** 12 (monthly)
*   **Time ($t$):** 3 years

The formula for compound interest is:
$$A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$$

Substitute the values:
$$A = 5000 \left(1 + \frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{12 \times 3}$$
$$A = 5000 \left(1 + \frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{36}$$
$$A = 5000 \left(1 + 0.003333...\right)^{36}$$
$$A = 5000 \left(1.003333...\right)^{36}$$
$$A = 5000 \times 1.1272727...$$
$$A = 5636.3636...$$

Rounding to the nearest cent:
**$5,636.36**

### 2. Flooring

**Working:**
*   **Total Area:** 1,850 sq ft
*   **Waste Factor:** 10%
*   **Total Area Needed:** $1,850 + (0.10 \times 1,850) = 1,850 + 185 = 2,035$ sq ft
*   **Box Size:** 23.5 sq ft

Calculate the number of boxes:
$$\text{Boxes} = \frac{\text{Total Area Needed}}{\text{Box Size}}$$
$$\text{Boxes} = \frac{2,035}{23.5}$$
$$\text{Boxes} = 86.5957...$$

Since you cannot buy a partial box, you must round up to the next whole number.
**87 boxes**

***

### 3. Gas Cost

**Working:**
*   **Distance:** 412 miles
*   **Miles per Gallon (MPG):** 34
*   **Price per Gallon:** $3.89

First, calculate the total gallons needed:
$$\text{Gallons} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{MPG}}$$
$$\text{Gallons} = \frac{412}{34}$$
$$\text{Gallons} = 12.117647...$$

Next, calculate the total cost:
$$\text{Cost} = \text{Gallons} \times \text{Price per Gallon}$$
$$\text{Cost} = 12.117647... \times 3.89$$
$$\text{Cost} = 47.1776...$$

Rounding to the nearest cent:
**$47.18**

***

### 4. Tip and Split

**Working:**
*   **Pre-tax Amount:** $172.00
*   **Tax Amount:** $186.40 - $172.00 = $14.40
*   **Tip Rate:** 18% on pre-tax amount

Calculate the tip:
$$\text{Tip} = 172.00 \times 0.18 = 30.96$$

Calculate the total bill:
$$\text{Total} = \text{Pre-tax} + \text{Tax} + \text{Tip}$$
$$\text{Total} = 172.00 + 14.40 + 30.96 = 217.36$$

Calculate the cost per person (split 4 ways):
$$\text{Cost per person} = \frac{217.36}{4}$$
$$\text{Cost per person} = 54.34$$

**$54.34**
