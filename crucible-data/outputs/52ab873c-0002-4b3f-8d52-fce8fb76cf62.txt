Each event has a well-established date:

- **Magna Carta** was sealed by King John at Runnymede in **1215**.
- **The Gutenberg Bible** was printed in Mainz around **1455**. Printing began a few years earlier, but copies were complete by about 1454–1455.
- **The French Revolution** began in **1789**, symbolized by the storming of the Bastille on 14 July.
- **The First World War** started in **1914**, following the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand.
- **The first Moon landing** was Apollo 11, on 20 July **1969**.
- **The Berlin Wall** fell on 9 November **1989**.

ORDER:
1. The signing of Magna Carta — 1215
2. The printing of the Gutenberg Bible — 1455
3. The start of the French Revolution — 1789
4. The start of the First World War — 1914
5. The first Moon landing — 1969
6. The fall of the Berlin Wall — 1989
