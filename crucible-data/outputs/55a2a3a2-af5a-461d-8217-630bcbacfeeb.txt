

These events can be ordered by well-known historical dates: Magna Carta was sealed in 1215; the Gutenberg Bible is usually dated to about 1455; the French Revolution began in 1789; the First World War began in 1914; the first Moon landing was in 1969; and the Berlin Wall fell in 1989.

ORDER:
1. The signing of Magna Carta — 1215
2. The printing of the Gutenberg Bible — 1455
3. The start of the French Revolution — 1789
4. The start of the First World War — 1914
5. The first Moon landing — 1969
6. The fall of the Berlin Wall — 1989
