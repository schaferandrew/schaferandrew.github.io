The Magna Carta was signed in 1215, establishing a foundation for constitutional law. The printing of the Gutenberg Bible followed in 1455, which revolutionized the spread of knowledge. The start of the French Revolution occurred in 1789, marking the end of the absolute monarchy in France. The First World War began in 1914, involving most of the world's major powers. The first Moon landing took place in 1969, a major achievement of the Space Age. Finally, the fall of the Berlin Wall happened in 1989, symbolizing the end of the Cold War.
