

## Assumptions / possible ambiguity

I will use the standard puzzle assumptions:

1. Chair 1 is leftmost and chair 4 is rightmost.
2. “Immediately to the left” means in the adjacent chair to the left.
3. “Somewhere to the right” means in a chair with a higher number; it may be immediately to the right.  
   - If the phrase were intended to mean *not* immediately to the right, the clues would have no solution.  
   - If it were intended to mean *immediately* to the right, the same solution results.
4. The four colors red, blue, green, and yellow are assigned one-to-one to Alice, Bob, Carol, and Dave.

## Reasoning

From the clues:

- Dave likes **red**.
- Carol likes **yellow**.
- The person who likes **green** sits in chair 1.

So the person in chair 1 cannot be Dave or Carol, because Dave is red and Carol is yellow. Therefore chair 1 must be either **Alice** or **Bob**.

That means Alice and Bob must be the two people assigned the remaining colors, **green** and **blue**.

Now use clue 1:

> Alice sits immediately to the left of the person who likes blue.

Alice cannot be the person who likes blue, because the blue person is to Alice’s right. Therefore Alice must be the person who likes **green**, and Bob must be the person who likes **blue**.

Since green is in chair 1:

- Alice is in chair 1.
- Bob, who likes blue, must be immediately to her right, so Bob is in chair 2.

The remaining chairs are 3 and 4, and the remaining people are Dave and Carol.

Clue 4 says Carol sits somewhere to the right of the person who likes red. Since Dave likes red, Carol must sit to the right of Dave. Therefore:

- Dave is in chair 3.
- Carol is in chair 4.

The full seating arrangement is:

| Chair | Person | Favorite color |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alice | green |
| 2 | Bob | blue |
| 3 | Dave | red |
| 4 | Carol | yellow |

## Final answer

The person who sits in chair 3 is **Dave**.
