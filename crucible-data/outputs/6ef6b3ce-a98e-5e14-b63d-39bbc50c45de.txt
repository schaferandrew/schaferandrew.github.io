**Daily Briefing: Port Ardmore – Tuesday, March 10**

**CITY COUNCIL APPROVES TRANSIT LEVY**
*Sources: Ardmore Ledger (7:05 a.m.), Lakeshore Public Radio (8:20 a.m.)*
The Port Ardmore City Council voted **7-2** late Monday to approve a **$41.6 million** transit levy spread over ten years. The funding will support three new bus routes and a rebuild of the Fulton Street station. Collection would begin in July if the levy passes its second reading on March 24.

*   **Council Vote:** Council member **Ines Varga**, who voted no, stated the city had not published a cost estimate for households.
*   **Mayor's Statement:** Mayor **Theo Brandt** called the approval "the biggest investment in buses this city has made in a generation."

**OPINION: LEVY CRITICISM**
*Source: The Ardmore Bugle (Opinion)*
Columnist **Rob Haddad** argues that the transit levy is a "tax grab." He contends the measure will cost a typical household **$900** a year and criticizes the council for rushing the vote, stating, "Nobody asked the people paying for it."

***

**KETTLE STREET WAREHOUSE FIRE UPDATE**
*Sources: Statewide Wire (6:10 a.m.), Lakeshore Public Radio (11:40 a.m.)*
A fire broke out early Tuesday at a vacant warehouse on Kettle Street in Port Ardmore. Crews had the fire under control by **9:15 a.m.**

*   **Initial Report:** An initial report from the scene stated that two firefighters were injured. The cause is under investigation.
*   **Correction/Update:** Fire Chief **Ama Mensah** later clarified that earlier reports of two injured firefighters were incorrect. Only one firefighter was treated for smoke inhalation and released. The cause remains under investigation.

***

**RENT STABILIZATION RALLY AT CITY HALL**
*Sources: Ardmore Ledger (9:30 a.m.), Lakeshore Public Radio (9:45 a.m.)*
Supporters of a proposed rent stabilization ordinance rallied outside City Hall on Monday evening. Estimates for the crowd size vary significantly between sources:

*   **Ledger Estimate:** Reporters at the scene estimated the crowd at about **300**.
*   **Organizer Claim:** Organizers said more than **1,000** people attended.
*   **Police Estimate:** Police estimated the crowd at **400**.

***

**TEACHER PAY BILL HEADS TO GOVERNOR**
*Source: Statewide Wire (10:00 a.m.)*
The state legislature passed a bill raising the minimum teacher salary to **$48,500**, by votes of **39-28** in the Senate and **81-52** in the House. Governor **Lena Ruiz** is expected to sign it this week, according to a spokesperson. The increase would take effect in the fall term.

***

**VELORIA CENTRAL BANK RATE HIKE**
*Source: Global Dispatch (5:50 a.m.)*
The central bank of Veloria raised its benchmark interest rate by **0.25 percentage points** to **3.75%**, its third increase this year, citing inflation of **4.1%**.
