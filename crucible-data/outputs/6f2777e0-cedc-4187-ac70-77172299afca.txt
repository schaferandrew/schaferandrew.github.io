Here is the chronological ordering of the events based on their historical dates:

1.  **The signing of Magna Carta** occurred in 1215 during the reign of King John of England.
2.  **The printing of the Gutenberg Bible** was completed around 1455, marking a pivotal moment in the history of mass communication.
3.  **The start of the French Revolution** is traditionally dated to 1789 with the storming of the Bastille.
4.  **The start of the First World War** began in 1914 following the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand.
5.  **The first Moon landing** took place in 1969 when Apollo 11 astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walked on the lunar surface.
6.  **The fall of the Berlin Wall** happened in 1989, symbolizing the end of the Cold War era in Europe.
