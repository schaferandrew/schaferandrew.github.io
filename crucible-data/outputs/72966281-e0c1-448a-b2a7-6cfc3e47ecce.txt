Here are the step-by-step calculations for each question.

### 1. Savings Account Compound Interest

**Formula:**
$$A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$$

Where:
*   $P = 5,000$ (Principal)
*   $r = 0.04$ (Annual interest rate)
*   $n = 12$ (Compounded monthly)
*   $t = 3$ (Years)

**Calculation:**
1.  Calculate the monthly interest rate: $\frac{0.04}{12} = 0.00333333...$
2.  Calculate the total number of compounding periods: $12 \times 3 = 36$
3.  Apply the formula:
    $$A = 5000 \times \left(1 + \frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{36}$$
    $$A = 5000 \times (1.00333333...)^{36}$$
    $$A = 5000 \times 1.12727182...$$
    $$A = 5636.3591...$$

4.  Round to the nearest cent:
    $$A \approx \$5,636.36$$

### 2. Flooring Boxes

**Step 1: Calculate total area needed including waste.**
*   Base area: $1,850$ sq ft
*   Waste factor: $10\%$ or $0.10$
*   Total area needed: $1,850 \times (1 + 0.10) = 1,850 \times 1.10 = 2,035$ sq ft

**Step 2: Calculate the number of boxes.**
*   Coverage per box: $23.5$ sq ft
*   Boxes needed: $\frac{2,035}{23.5}$
*   Calculation: $2,035 \div 23.5 \approx 86.5957...$

**Step 3: Determine final purchase amount.**
Since you cannot buy a fraction of a box, you must round up to the next whole number to ensure you have enough flooring.
*   Boxes to buy: $87$

### 3. Road Trip Gas Cost

**Step 1: Calculate gallons of gas needed.**
*   Distance: $412$ miles
*   Fuel efficiency: $34$ miles per gallon
*   Gallons needed: $\frac{412}{34}$
*   Calculation: $412 \div 34 \approx 12.117647...$ gallons

**Step 2: Calculate total cost.**
*   Price per gallon: $\$3.89$
*   Total cost: $12.117647... \times 3.89$
*   Calculation: $47.137647...$

**Step 3: Round to the nearest cent.**
*   Total cost: $\$47.14$

### 4. Dinner Bill Split

**Step 1: Calculate the tip amount.**
*   Pre-tax amount: $\$172.00$
*   Tip percentage: $18\%$ or $0.18$
*   Tip: $172.00 \times 0.18 = 30.96$

**Step 2: Calculate the total bill.**
*   Pre-tax amount: $\$172.00$
*   Tax (included in total): $\$186.40 - \$172.00 = \$14.40$ (Note: We use the given total including tax directly)
*   Tip: $\$30.96$
*   Total Bill: $186.40 (\text{with tax}) + 30.96 (\text{tip}) = 217.36$

**Step 3: Split among four people.**
*   Total Bill: $\$217.36$
*   Number of people: $4$
*   Share per person: $\frac{217.36}{4}$
*   Calculation: $54.34$

*Note: Since the division results in an exact cent value, no further rounding is needed.*
