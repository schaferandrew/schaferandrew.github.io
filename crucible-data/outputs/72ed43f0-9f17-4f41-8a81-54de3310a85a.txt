**[S1]**  
CITY COUNCIL APPROVES TRANSIT LEVY. The Port Ardmore City Council voted 7-2 late Monday to approve a $41.6 million transit levy spread over ten years. The money will fund three new bus routes and a rebuild of the Fulton Street station. Council member Ines Varga, who voted no, said the city had not published a cost estimate for households. The levy still needs a second reading on March 24.

**[S2]**  
PORT ARDOR'S COUNCIL BACKED THE TRANSIT LEVY ON A 7-2 VOTE. Mayor Theo Brandt called it "the biggest investment in buses this city has made in a generation." Collection would begin July 1 if the levy passes its second reading.

**[S3]**  
WAREHOUSE FIRE ON KETTLE STREET. A fire broke out early Tuesday at a vacant warehouse on Kettle Street in Port Ardmore. Two firefighters were injured, according to an initial report from the scene. The cause is under investigation.

**[S4]**  
UPDATE, Kettle Street fire: Fire Chief Ama Mensah said one firefighter was treated for smoke inhalation and released; earlier reports that two firefighters were injured were incorrect. Crews had the fire under control by 9:15 a.m. The cause remains under investigation.

**[S5]**  
RALLY AT CITY HALL. Supporters of a proposed rent stabilization ordinance rallied outside City Hall on Monday evening. Ledger reporters at the scene estimated the crowd at about 300.

**[S6]**  
ORGANIZERS OF MONDAY'S RENT STABILIZATION RALLY SAY MORE THAN 1,000 PEOPLE ATTENDED. Police estimated the crowd at 400.

**[S7]**  
TEACHER PAY BILL HEADS TO GOVERNOR. The state legislature passed a bill raising the minimum teacher salary to $48,500, by votes of 39-28 in the Senate and 81-52 in the House. Governor Lena Ruiz is expected to sign it this week, a spokesperson said. The increase would take effect in the fall term.
