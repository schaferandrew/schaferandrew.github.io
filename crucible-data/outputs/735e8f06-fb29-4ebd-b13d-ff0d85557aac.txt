# Analysis of the Letter (dated June 10, 1863)

**a) Emancipation Proclamation taking effect this New Year's Day** — ✅ Known. It took effect **January 1, 1863**, months before this letter.

**b) Siege of Vicksburg dragging on** — ✅ Known. The siege ran **May 25 – July 4, 1863**, so it was ongoing on June 10.

**c) News of Lee's defeat at Gettysburg** — ❌ Impossible. The battle was fought **July 1–3, 1863** — nearly a month in the *future*.

**d) The telegraph bringing news from Washington** — ✅ Known. The telegraph network was well established by the Civil War.

**e) The Monitor's fight with the Merrimack last year** — ✅ Known. That battle (Hampton Roads) occurred **March 1862**.

**f) A cousin in Cleveland who telephoned** — ❌ Impossible. The telephone wasn't invented until **1876** (Bell).

**g) Fencing the north pasture with barbed wire** — ❌ Impossible. Barbed wire wasn't patented until **1874** (Glidden).

**h) Mathew Brady's photographs of the dead at Antietam** — ✅ Known. Antietam was **September 1862**, and Brady's photos were exhibited in late 1862.

**i) Stonewall Jackson's death last month** — ✅ Known. "The Rock Wall" died **May 10, 1863** (wounds), fitting "last month."

**j) West Virginia joining the Union last month** — ❌ Impossible. West Virginia was admitted **June 20, 1863** — still ~10 days away, and not in May as claimed.

**k) The draft riots in New York** — ❌ Impossible. Those riots occurred **July 13–16, 1863**, weeks in the future.

ANACHRONISMS: c, f, g, j, k
