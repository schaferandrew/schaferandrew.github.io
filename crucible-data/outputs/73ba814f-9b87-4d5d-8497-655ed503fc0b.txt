**Answers**

1. None – no sovereign state lies on both the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn.  
2. Saudi Arabia – it has coastlines along both the Red Sea (south) and the Persian Gulf (east).  
3. Reykjavík – at about 64° N, it is the northernmost capital city of any independent country.  
4. France – its mainland plus overseas territories cover 12 distinct time zones worldwide.  
5. Alaska – the state’s westernmost point is farthest from Africa, giving the shortest great‑circle distance to the continent.  
6. Lesotho – this landlocked nation borders only South Africa and no other country.

---  

**JUSTIFICATIONS**

1. The Equator (0° latitude) crosses countries in Central/South America and Africa, while the Tropic of Capricorn (~23.5° S) passes through southern Africa and parts of Oceania; their latitudinal ranges do not overlap for any single sovereign state.  
2. Saudi Arabia’s northern coastline lies on the Red Sea, and its eastern coast borders the Persian Gulf, making it unique in this regard.  
3. Among all capital cities, Reykjavík sits at the highest latitude (≈64° N), surpassing Ulaanbaatar (≈47° N) and others.  
4. France’s overseas departments and territories—such as French Polynesia, New Caledonia, Réunion, and others—extend its time‑zone coverage to 12 distinct zones, more than any other country including its mainland alone.  
5. The shortest great‑circle route from the United States to Africa runs from Alaska (near the Bering Strait) to West Africa, making Alaska the closest U.S. state to mainland Africa.  
6. Lesotho is entirely surrounded by South Africa and shares no borders with any other nation, fitting the criterion of a landlocked country that borders exactly one sovereign state.
