a) The Emancipation Proclamation was issued September 22, 1862 and took effect January 1, 1863. By June 10 the farmer could know it had taken effect.

b) The Siege of Vicksburg began May 18 and ended July 4, 1863. On June 10 it was ongoing, so this was known.

c) Gettysburg ended July 3, 1863. In June 1863 Lee's army was still intact; the farmer could not know about the defeat.

d) The telegraph existed and was used during the war. News from Washington via telegraph was possible.

e) The Monitor vs Merrimack battle occurred January 29, 1863. By June 10 this historical event was known.

f) The telephone was invented in 1876. A cousin could not have telephoned in 1863.

g) Barbed wire was developed around 1874. In June 1863 the farmer could not be fencing with it.

h) Mathew Brady photographed Antietam in September 1862, and photos were published shortly after. By June 10 these images would have been available.

i) Stonewall Jackson died May 2, 1863. By June 10 this news would be known.

j) West Virginia became a state December 1863. In June it was still a territory; the farmer could not know it had joined the Union "last month."

k) The New York Draft Riots occurred July 13-25, 1863. In early June they had not yet happened.
