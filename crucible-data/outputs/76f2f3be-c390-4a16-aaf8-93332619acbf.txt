

Here is the breakdown of what a farmer in Ohio could and could not have known or done on June 10, 1863:

**a) The Emancipation Proclamation taking effect this New Year's Day**
*Possible.* The proclamation took effect on January 1, 1863. By June, this was well-known history.

**b) The siege of Vicksburg dragging on**
*Possible.* The Siege of Vicksburg began in May 1863 and did not end until July 4, 1863. On June 10, it was still active.

**c) News of Lee's defeat at Gettysburg**
*Impossible (Anachronism).* The Battle of Gettysburg took place from July 1 to July 3, 1863. The writer could not know about a battle that had not yet occurred.

**d) The telegraph bringing news from Washington**
*Possible.* The transcontinental telegraph was completed in 1861, and news traveled rapidly via this method by 1863.

**e) The Monitor's fight with the Merrimack last year**
*Possible.* This naval battle occurred on March 8–9, 1862. "Last year" relative to 1863 refers to 1862, so this is accurate.

**f) A cousin in Cleveland who telephoned with the news**
*Impossible (Anachronism).* The telephone was not invented until 1876, more than a decade after this letter was written.

**g) Fencing the north pasture with barbed wire**
*Impossible (Anachronism).* While primitive versions existed, the patent for barbed wire was granted in 1874, and it did not become a common farming tool until the late 1860s.

**h) Mathew Brady's photographs of the dead at Antietam**
*Possible.* The Battle of Antietam occurred in September 1862. Photographs of the aftermath were widely circulated by early 1863.

**i) Stonewall Jackson's death last month**
*Possible.* General Jackson died on May 10, 1863. Since the letter is dated June 10, "last month" accurately refers to his death in May.

**j) West Virginia, which joined the Union last month**
*Impossible (Anachronism).* West Virginia was admitted to the Union on June 20, 1863. On June 10, it had not yet joined the Union.

**k) The draft riots in New York**
*Impossible (Anachronism).* The New York City Draft Riots occurred from July 13 to July 16, 1863. The writer could not know about events that happened a month later.

ANACHRONISMS: c, f, g, j, k
