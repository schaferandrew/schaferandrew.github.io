Gabon is crossed by the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn? Wait—the Tropic of Capricorn does not cross Gabon; it crosses countries farther south. No country is crossed by both, since the Equator and Tropic of Capricorn are in opposite hemispheres.

Saudi Arabia borders the Red Sea and the Persian Gulf. Reykjavík is the northernmost capital of a sovereign state. France spans the most time zones when overseas territories are included. Maine is the closest US state to mainland Africa. Portugal is not landlocked, so the final question’s premise appears to be erroneous: Uzbekistan and Liechtenstein are the landlocked countries that border exactly one other country? Uzbekistan borders five countries, while Liechtenstein borders Austria and Switzerland. In fact, no such country exists apart from the Italian microstates.
