1. **3809**
To find the number of integers from 1 to 10,000 divisible by 3 or 7 but not by 21, we use the Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion.
- Multiples of 3: $\lfloor 10000/3 \rfloor = 3333$
- Multiples of 7: $\lfloor 10000/7 \rfloor = 1428$
- Multiples of 21 (divisible by both): $\lfloor 10000/21 \rfloor = 476$
The count of numbers divisible by 3 or 7 is $3333 + 1428 - 476 = 4285$. Since all multiples of 21 are included in this count, we subtract them to satisfy the "not by 21" condition: $4285 - 476 = 3809$.

2. **49**
We calculate $7^{2026} \pmod{100}$. The powers of 7 modulo 100 repeat with a period of 4:
- $7^1 \equiv 07$
- $7^2 \equiv 49$
- $7^3 \equiv 343 \equiv 43$
- $7^4 \equiv 2401 \equiv 01$
Since $2026 \equiv 2 \pmod 4$, $7^{2026} \equiv 7^2 \equiv 49 \pmod{100}$.

3. **2731**
Let $a_n$ be the number of ways to tile a $2 \times n$ board. The recurrence relation is $a_n = a_{n-1} + 2a_{n-2}$ (placing a vertical domino, two horizontal dominoes, or one square).
- $a_0 = 1, a_1 = 1$
- $a_2 = 3, a_3 = 5, a_4 = 11, a_5 = 21, a_6 = 43, a_7 = 85, a_8 = 171, a_9 = 341, a_{10} = 683, a_{11} = 1365, a_{12} = 2731$.
Alternatively, $a_n = \frac{2^{n+1} + (-1)^n}{3}$. For $n=12$, $a_{12} = \frac{8192 + 1}{3} = 2731$.

4. **37/216**
The total number of outcomes for three dice is $6^3 = 216$. The largest number is exactly 4 if all dice show $\le 4$ and at least one shows 4.
- Outcomes with all dice $\le 4$: $4^3 = 64$.
- Outcomes with all dice $\le 3$: $3^3 = 27$.
- Outcomes with max exactly 4: $64 - 27 = 37$.
The probability is $37/216$.

5. **13/27**
Let $B$ be the event both children are boys, and $T$ be the event at least one is a boy born on Tuesday.
- $P(B) = 1/4$.
- $P(T|B)$: Given two boys, the probability at least one is born on Tuesday is $1 - (6/7)^2 = 13/49$.
- $P(T)$: The probability at least one child is a boy born on Tuesday is $1 - (13/14)^2 = 27/196$.
Using Bayes' theorem: $P(B|T) = \frac{P(T|B)P(B)}{P(T)} = \frac{(13/49)(1/4)}{27/196} = \frac{13/196}{27/196} = 13/27$.
