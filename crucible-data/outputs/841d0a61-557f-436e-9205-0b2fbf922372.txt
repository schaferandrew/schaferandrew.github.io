

1. Brazil is crossed by both the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn because it stretches from equatorial Amazonia to the subtropical south.
2. Saudi Arabia has coastline on both the Red Sea and the Persian Gulf, with the Red Sea on its west and the Persian Gulf on its east.
3. Reykjavík is the northernmost capital city of a sovereign state because it lies at about 64°N and no independent country’s capital is farther north.
4. France spans the most time zones when overseas territories are counted because its Pacific, Indian Ocean, Caribbean, and Atlantic possessions give it more civil time zones than Russia’s eleven.
5. Maine is the closest US state to mainland Africa because its far northeastern coast is nearer to Morocco than any point in any other US state is to mainland Africa.
6. Lesotho is the only landlocked country other than Italy’s microstates that borders exactly one country because it is entirely surrounded by South Africa.

ANSWERS
1. Brazil
2. Saudi Arabia
3. Reykjavík
4. France
5. Maine
6. Lesotho
