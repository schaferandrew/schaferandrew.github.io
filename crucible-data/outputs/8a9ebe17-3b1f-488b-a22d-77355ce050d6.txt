**Problem 4:** Three fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the largest number shown is exactly 4?

For the largest to be exactly 4, all dice must show numbers from {1,2,3,4} and at least one die must show 4.

Total outcomes: 6^3 = 216

Outcomes where all dice show numbers from {1,2,3,4}: 4^3 = 64
Outcomes where all dice show numbers from {1,2,3}: 3^3 = 27

So outcomes where largest is exactly 4: 64 - 27 = 37

Probability = 37/216
