**1. Savings Account Calculation**
To find the future value of the savings, we use the compound interest formula: $A = P(1 + \frac{r}{n})^{nt}$
*   **P (Principal):** $5,000
*   **r (Annual Rate):** 0.04
*   **n (Compounds per year):** 12
*   **t (Years):** 3

$$A = 5000 \left(1 + \frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{(12 \times 3)}$$
$$A = 5000 (1 + 0.003333...)^{36}$$
$$A = 5000 (1.127498...)^{36}$$
$$A = 5000 (1.127498... \text{ [calculated precisely]})$$
*Note: $(1 + 0.04/12)^{36} \approx 1.12749805$*
$$A = 5000 \times 1.12749805...$$
$$A = 5637.49025...$$

Rounding to the nearest cent: **$5,637.49**

***

**2. Flooring Calculation**
First, calculate the total area needed including the 10% waste factor.
*   **Base Area:** 1,850 sq ft
*   **Waste Factor:** 1.10 (100% + 10%)

$$\text{Total Area Needed} = 1850 \times 1.10 = 2,035 \text{ sq ft}$$

Next, divide the total area by the size of one box to find the number of boxes.
*   **Box Size:** 23.5 sq ft

$$\text{Boxes} = \frac{2035}{23.5}$$
$$\text{Boxes} = 86.5957...$$

Since you cannot buy a partial box, you must round up to the next whole number.
**87 boxes**

***

**3. Gas Cost Calculation**
First, determine how many gallons of gas are needed for the trip.
*   **Distance:** 412 miles
*   **MPG:** 34

$$\text{Gallons} = \frac{412}{34}$$
$$\text{Gallons} = 12.117647...$$

Next, calculate the cost using the price per gallon.
*   **Price:** $3.89

$$\text{Cost} = 12.117647... \times 3.89$$
$$\text{Cost} = 47.1376...$$

Rounding to the nearest cent: **$47.14**

***

**4. Dinner Split Calculation**
First, calculate the tip amount based on the pre-tax total.
*   **Pre-tax:** $172.00
*   **Tip Rate:** 18% (0.18)

$$\text{Tip} = 172 \times 0.18 = 30.96$$

Next, add the tip to the total bill (which already includes tax) to find the final amount to be split.
*   **Total with Tax:** $186.40

$$\text{Final Total} = 186.40 + 30.96 = 217.36$$

Finally, divide by the number of people (4).
*   **People:** 4

$$\text{Share per person} = \frac{217.36}{4}$$
$$\text{Share per person} = 54.34$$

**$54.34**
