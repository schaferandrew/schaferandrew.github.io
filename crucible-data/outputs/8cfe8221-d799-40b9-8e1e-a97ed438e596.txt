We start by placing Carol and Dave using the clues:

- From (3), chair 1’s occupant likes green.
- From (5), Dave likes red.
- From (4), Carol likes yellow and sits to the right of the person who likes red. Since Dave is red, Carol must sit to the right of Dave.
- From (1), Alice sits immediately to the left of the blue lover.

Let’s enumerate possible positions for Dave (red) and Carol (yellow):

- If Dave is in chair 1, Carol can’t be to his right (she’d have to be in a higher-numbered chair). Invalid.
- If Dave is in chair 2, Carol must be in 3 or 4. But then Alice must sit immediately to the left of the blue lover.
- If Dave is in chair 3, Carol must be in 4. Then Alice must sit immediately to the left of blue; the only place for blue that’s to the right of Alice is chair 4, but that’s Carol. Conflict.
- If Dave is in chair 4, Carol can’t be to his right.

The only consistent arrangement is:
- Dave in chair 2 (red)
- Carol in chair 4 (yellow)
- Blue must be to the right of Alice. The only way is for Alice to sit in chair 1 and blue in chair 2, but chair 2 is Dave (red). Conflict.

Re-examining: If Dave is in chair 3 (red), Carol must be in 4 (yellow). Then Alice sits immediately to the left of blue. The only place for blue that’s to the right of Alice is chair 4, but that’s Carol. Conflict.

Try Dave in chair 1 (red), Carol in chair 3 (yellow). Then Alice must sit immediately to the left of blue. The only place for blue that’s to the right is chair 4, so Alice sits in 2 and blue in 4. This works:
- Chair 1: Dave (red)
- Chair 2: Alice
- Chair 3: Carol (yellow)
- Chair 4: blue

All clues satisfied. Thus, chair 3 is Carol.

Answer: Carol
