### 1. Savings account
For monthly compounding,
\[
A=P\left(1+\frac{r}{12}\right)^{12t}.
\]
Substituting:
\[
A=5000\left(1+\frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{36}.
\]
Rounding only the final result to the nearest cent gives **$5,636.36**.

### 2. Flooring boxes
Include 10% extra for waste:
\[
1850(1.10)=2035\text{ sq ft}.
\]
The number of boxes required is
\[
\frac{2035}{23.5}=\frac{4070}{47}.
\]
Since you must buy whole boxes, round **up** to **87 boxes**.

### 3. Gas cost
Gallons needed:
\[
\frac{412}{34}\text{ gallons}.
\]
Total cost:
\[
\frac{412}{34}\times 3.89=\frac{1602.68}{34}.
\]
Rounding to the nearest cent gives **$47.14**.

### 4. Dinner split
Tip on the pre-tax amount:
\[
172.00(0.18)=\$30.96.
\]
Total including tax and tip:
\[
186.40+30.96=\$217.36.
\]
Each person pays:
\[
\frac{217.36}{4}=\boxed{\$54.34}.
\]

## ANSWERS
1. $5,636.36
2. 87 boxes
3. $47.14
4. $54.34
