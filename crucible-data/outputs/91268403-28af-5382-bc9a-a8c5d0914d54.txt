### Objective errors
- **“one of the two items were damaged”** → **“one of the two items was damaged”**. The subject is *one*.
- **“havent”** → **“haven’t”**. The contraction needs an apostrophe.
- **“was clearly crush”** → **“was clearly crushed”**.
- Add a comma before **“but”** in “I contacted support last week, but I haven’t heard anything back,” since it joins two independent clauses.

### Optional style and clarity improvements
- Use an en dash in **“5–7 business days”**; this is a typographic preference.
- **“which seem to have passed already for my earlier request”** is understandable but awkward. **“that timeframe seems to have passed since my earlier request”** is clearer.

### Corrected version

**Subject: Refund for order 84721**

Hi Support,

I am writing because my order arrived three days late, and one of the two items was damaged. I contacted support last week, but I haven’t heard anything back. The box was clearly crushed on one side, and the mug inside has a crack through the handle. I would like a refund for the mug, but please let me know if you need pictures of it. Also, your website says refunds are processed in 5–7 business days, and that timeframe seems to have passed since my earlier request. Can you tell me what the status is? Thanks for your help.
