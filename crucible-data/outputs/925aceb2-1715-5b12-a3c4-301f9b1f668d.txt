**c) News of Lee's defeat at Gettysburg:** Gettysburg did not occur until July 1–3, 1863. On June 10th, the battle was still a month away.

**g) Fencing the north pasture with barbed wire:** While the concept existed, practical, mass-produced barbed wire was not commonly available or in widespread use on farms until after the war (mid-to-late 1860s).

**i) Stonewall Jackson's death last month:** Jackson died on June 25, 1863. If the letter was written on June 10th, it would be impossible for his death to have occurred "last month" (May 1863).
