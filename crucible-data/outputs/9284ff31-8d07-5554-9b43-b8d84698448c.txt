**Port Ardmore Briefing: Tuesday, March 10**

**Transit Levy Approved by City Council**
The Port Ardmore City Council voted 7-2 late Monday to approve a $41.6 million transit levy spread over ten years (Ardmore Ledger). The money will fund three new bus routes and a rebuild of the Fulton Street station (Ardmore Ledger). Mayor Theo Brandt called it "the biggest investment in buses this city has made in a generation" (Lakeshore Public Radio). Collection would begin July 1 if the levy passes its second reading (Lakeshore Public Radio). The levy still needs a second reading on March 24 (Ardmore Ledger). Council member Ines Varga, who voted no, said the city had not published a cost estimate for households (Ardmore Ledger).

*Opinion:* Columnist Rob Haddad argues that the transit levy will cost a typical household $900 a year and that the council rushed the vote. He writes, "Nobody asked the people paying for it" (The Ardmore Bugle).

**Warehouse Fire on Kettle Street**
A fire broke out early Tuesday at a vacant warehouse on Kettle Street in Port Ardmore (Statewide Wire). Crews had the fire under control by 9:15 a.m. (Lakeshore Public Radio). The cause is under investigation (Statewide Wire; Lakeshore Public Radio).

*Injury Report:* An initial report from the scene stated two firefighters were injured (Statewide Wire). Fire Chief Ama Mensah said one firefighter was treated for smoke inhalation and released; earlier reports that two firefighters were injured were incorrect (Lakeshore Public Radio).

**Rent Stabilization Rally**
Supporters of a proposed rent stabilization ordinance rallied outside City Hall on Monday evening (Ardmore Ledger). Ledger reporters at the scene estimated the crowd at about 300 (Ardmore Ledger). Organizers of Monday's rent stabilization rally said more than 1,000 people attended (Lakeshore Public Radio). Police estimated the crowd at 400 (Lakeshore Public Radio).

**Teacher Pay Bill Heads to Governor**
The state legislature passed a bill raising the minimum teacher salary to $48,500 (Statewide Wire). The vote was 39-28 in the Senate and 81-52 in the House (Statewide Wire). Governor Lena Ruiz is expected to sign it this week, a spokesperson said (Statewide Wire). The increase would take effect in the fall term (Statewide Wire).
