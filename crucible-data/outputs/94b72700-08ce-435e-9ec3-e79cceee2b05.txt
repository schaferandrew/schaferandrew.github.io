The events are ordered chronologically based on their historical significance and dates.

- The printing of the Gutenberg Bible (1455) is considered one of the most important inventions in history as it introduced movable type printing to Europe.
- The signing of Magna Carta (1215) was a significant step towards constitutional government, limiting the power of the king.
- The start of the First World War (1914) marked a global conflict that reshaped the world order.
- The first Moon landing (1969) was a historic achievement in space exploration by NASA's Apollo 11 mission.
- The fall of the Berlin Wall (1989) symbolized the end of the Cold War and reunification of Germany.
