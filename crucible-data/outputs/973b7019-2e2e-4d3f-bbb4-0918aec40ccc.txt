**Reasoning**

1. **Clue 3** tells us the person who likes green sits in chair 1, so  
   `Chair 1 = color green`.

2. **Clues 4 and 5** give fixed colors for two names:  
   - Dave’s favourite colour is red.  
   - Carol’s favourite colour is yellow.

3. The remaining colours are blue and green.  
   Since chair 1 already has green, the person there must be either Alice or Bob (the only names that can have green).  
   Consequently the other of Alice/Bob will have blue.

4. **Clue 1**: “Alice sits immediately to the left of the person who likes blue.”  
   This gives three positional possibilities:

   | Alice’s chair | Blue‑person’s chair |
   |---------------|---------------------|
   | 1             | 2                   |
   | 2             | 3                   |
   | 3             | 4                   |

5. **Clue 2**: Bob cannot sit in chair 4.

6. **Clue 4** (re‑phrased): “Carol sits somewhere to the right of the person who likes red.”  
   Since Dave is the only one who likes red, Dave must be seated left of Carol.

---

### Checking the possibilities

| Possibility | Alice | Blue‑person | Remaining names for chairs 3 & 4 | Feasible? |
|-------------|-------|-------------|---------------------------------|-----------|
| **A** | chair 1 | chair 2 | Dave (red) at 3, Carol (yellow) at 4 | ✔︎ |
| B | chair 2 | chair 3 | would require blue‑person to be Dave or Carol – impossible | ✘ |
| C | chair 3 | chair 4 | would put blue on a chair that must hold Dave or Carol – impossible | ✘ |

Only possibility **A** satisfies all constraints.

---

### Final seating

- Chair 1: Alice, green  
- Chair 2: Bob, blue  
- Chair 3: Dave, red  
- Chair 4: Carol, yellow  

All clues are met:

* Alice is immediately left of the blue‑person (Bob).  
* Bob is not in chair 4.  
* Green sits in chair 1.  
* Carol (yellow) is to the right of Dave (red).  
* Dave likes red.
