**Port Ardmore – Tuesday, March 10 Briefing**

1. **Transit Levy Approved by City Council (7‑2)**
   - *Sources:* Ardmore Ledger (7:05 a.m.), Lakeshore Public Radio (8:20 a.m.)  
   - The council voted 7‑2 to approve a $41.6 million transit levy that will be spread over ten years. Funds are earmarked for three new bus routes and the rebuild of the Fulton Street station.  
   - Mayor Theo Brandt called it “the biggest investment in buses this city has made in a generation.”  
   - The levy still requires a second reading on March 24; collection would begin July 1 if that passes.

2. **Kettle Street Warehouse Fire**
   - *Sources:* Statewide Wire (6:10 a.m.), Lakeshore Public Radio (11:40 a.m.)  
   - A fire broke out early Tuesday at a vacant warehouse on Kettle Street. Initial reports said two firefighters were injured, but the fire chief later clarified that only one firefighter was treated for smoke inhalation and released; earlier injury reports were incorrect. The fire was under control by 9:15 a.m., and its cause remains under investigation.

3. **Rent‑Stabilization Rally Outside City Hall**
   - *Sources:* Ardmore Ledger (9:30 a.m.), Lakeshore Public Radio (9:45 a.m.)  
   - Supporters of a proposed rent‑stabilization ordinance rallied outside City Hall on Monday evening. Ledger reporters estimated the crowd at about 300, while organizers claimed more than 1,000 people attended. Police estimated the crowd at 400.

4. **State Teacher Pay Bill Advances to Governor**
   - *Source:* Statewide Wire (10:00 a.m.)  
   - The state legislature passed a bill raising the minimum teacher salary to $48,500 by votes of 39‑28 in the Senate and 81‑52 in the House. A governor’s spokesperson said Governor Lena Ruiz is expected to sign it this week; the increase would take effect in the fall term.

5. **Veloria Central Bank Raises Benchmark Rate**
   - *Source:* Global Dispatch (5:50 a.m.)  
   - The central bank of Veloria increased its benchmark interest rate by 0.25 percentage points to 3.75%, its third rise this year, citing an inflation rate of 4.1%.

6. **Opinion Piece on the Transit Levy**
   - *Source:* Ardmore Bugle (opinion, March 10)  
   - Columnist Rob Haddad argues that the transit levy will cost a typical household $900 per year and claims the council rushed the vote without adequate public input.

*All figures, names, and statuses are taken directly from the cited sources. No additional background has been added.*
