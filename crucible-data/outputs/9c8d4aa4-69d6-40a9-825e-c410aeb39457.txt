**1.**  

Future value formula for compound interest:  

\[
A = P\left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}
\]

- \(P = \$5{,}000\)  
- annual nominal rate \(r = 4\% = 0.04\)  
- compounding monthly ⇒ \(n = 12\)  
- time \(t = 3\) years  

\[
A = 5000\left(1+\frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{12\cdot 3}
     = 5000\left(1+ \frac{1}{300}\right)^{36}
\]

\[
\left(1+\frac{1}{300}\right)^{36}=e^{36\ln(1+1/300)}\approx e^{0.11980044}\approx 1.12727185
\]

\[
A \approx 5000 \times 1.12727185 = 5{,}636.3592595
\]

Rounded to the nearest cent: **\$5,636.36**.
