# Everyday Money and Measurement Answers

## 1. Savings account with monthly compounding

Formula: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)

- P = 5,000
- r = 0.04
- n = 12
- t = 3

Monthly rate: 0.04 / 12 = 0.003333…

Number of periods: 12 × 3 = 36

Growth factor: (1.003333…)^36

- ln(1.003333…) ≈ 0.00332779009
- × 36 = 0.1198004432
- e^0.1198004432 ≈ 1.1272719

A = 5,000 × 1.1272719 ≈ 5,636.359…

Rounded to the cent: **$5,636.36**

## 2. Flooring boxes

- Area with 10% waste: 1,850 × 1.10 = 2,035 sq ft
- Boxes needed: 2,035 ÷ 23.5 = 86.596…

You can't buy part of a box, so round up: **87 boxes**

## 3. Road-trip gas cost

- Gallons used: 412 ÷ 34 = 12.117647… gal
- Cost: 12.117647… × $3.89 = (412 × 3.89) ÷ 34 = 1,602.68 ÷ 34 = $47.13765…

Rounded to the cent: **$47.14**

## 4. Dinner split

- Tip, 18% of the pre-tax amount: 0.18 × $172.00 = $30.96
- Total: $186.40 + $30.96 = $217.36
- Per person: $217.36 ÷ 4 = **$54.34**

---

## ANSWERS

1. $5,636.36
2. 87 boxes
3. $47.14
4. $54.34 per person
