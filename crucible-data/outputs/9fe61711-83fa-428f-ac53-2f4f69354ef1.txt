1. Brazil is the only country whose territory extends from the Equator southward across the Tropic of Capricorn.  
2. Saudi Arabia is the only country with coastlines on both the Red Sea (west) and the Persian Gulf (east).  
3. Reykjavík is the northernmost capital of a sovereign state, as Nuuk is only the capital of Greenland, a non-sovereign territory.  
4. France spans the most time zones (12) when its overseas departments and territories are included, exceeding Russia’s 11.  
5. Maine is the closest US state to mainland Africa because its easternmost point yields the shortest great-circle distance to northwestern Africa.  
6. Lesotho is the only landlocked country that borders exactly one other country (South Africa), apart from San Marino and Vatican City inside Italy.  

ANSWERS  
1. Brazil  
2. Saudi Arabia  
3. Reykjavík  
4. France  
5. Maine  
6. Lesotho
