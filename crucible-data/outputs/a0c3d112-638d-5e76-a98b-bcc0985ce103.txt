### **2. Minnesota Voters Face School Funding Decisions**

**What Happened:** On Nov. 3, Minnesotans in Ramsey and Dakota counties will vote on multiple school levies plus a statewide constitutional amendment to increase per-pupil funding from the Permanent School Fund. The amendment would provide ~$900,000 annually to St. Paul Public Schools without raising taxes, addressing years of underfunding. Local referendums include North St. Paul-Maplewood-Oakdale's $800/pupil general education increase (10 years), West St. Paul-Mendota Heights-Eagan's capital project levy (~$4.6M for safety, textbooks, transportation), and proposals in Inver Grove Heights and Randolph districts.

**Why It Matters:** These decisions directly impact district budgets and student resources. The constitutional amendment represents the first time in decades that Minnesota can draw more on its school trust fund, which has grown from $675M (2010) to over $2.3B but distributions lag due to outdated formulas. St. Paul Superintendent Stacie Stanley calls it essential for maintaining public education quality.

*Sources: Pioneer Press | Twin Cities | Imani Cruzen / icruzen@pioneerpress.com | Reported facts include ballot language and funding amounts; analysis based on district statements and state constitutional history.*
