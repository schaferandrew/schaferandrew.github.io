Both puzzles were solved by matching the crossing letters of the long words first, then filling the short slots from the leftover words.

**Puzzle A**

- The four 7-letter slots (4-ACROSS, 4-DOWN, 5-DOWN, 12-ACROSS) form a loop of shared corner letters. 4-ACROSS and 4-DOWN share a first letter, so they must be COUNCIL and COURAGE. Their last letters then force LIBRARY and ECONOMY into 5-DOWN and 12-ACROSS.
- If 4-ACROSS is COURAGE, then 7-ACROSS and 10-ACROSS both need PROBE, which is impossible. So 4-ACROSS = COUNCIL, 4-DOWN = COURAGE, 5-DOWN = LIBRARY and 12-ACROSS = ECONOMY.
- 2-DOWN has C as its third letter, which gives UNCLE. It starts 2-ACROSS with U (UPSET) and ends in E, so 7-ACROSS starts with E (ELBOW). 3-DOWN starts with T and ends in W, giving THREW.
- 9-DOWN has O as its third letter (from ECONOMY), giving PROBE. That makes 8-ACROSS SWAMP and 10-ACROSS DEALT.
- DEALT ends in T, so 11-DOWN is TWIST. PROBE ends in E, so 13-ACROSS is DRIVE, and 8-DOWN (S…D) is SQUAD.
- 6-ACROSS needs U as its third letter, giving COUNT. That leaves SHIRT for 14-ACROSS (ending in T), and CUBIC (1-DOWN, ending in C) and CANDY (1-ACROSS).

**Puzzle B**

- 17-ACROSS and 17-DOWN share a first letter, and 17-DOWN must start with A, so it is ANOTHER or APRICOT. The ALIKE/IMAGINE option fails because 25-ACROSS would need to start with I. So 17-ACROSS = APPLY, 18-DOWN = PLASTIC and 20-ACROSS = AROMA. That makes 17-DOWN ANOTHER, and 22-ACROSS (ending in H) PUBLISH.
- 27-ACROSS has R as its third letter and C as its fifth, giving APRICOT. Then 24-DOWN has A as its third letter, giving IMAGINE, and 23-DOWN is BARGAIN.
- 29-ACROSS is ALIKE (A at column 3, I at column 5) and 31-ACROSS is OUNCE.
- 19-DOWN ends in P, so it is TOP. 26-ACROSS ends in R. If it were TAR, 26-DOWN would be a fifth T-word, and only four T-words exist. So 26-ACROSS = CAR and 26-DOWN = COG.
- 30-ACROSS and 30-DOWN share a first letter, so they are ANY and APT. Only ANY across works, because 28-DOWN would otherwise have to end in T. So 30-ACROSS = ANY, 30-DOWN = APT, 28-DOWN = TOY, and 32-ACROSS starts with T.
- The remaining crossings force 16-DOWN = FAN, 19-ACROSS = TEN, 15-ACROSS = ELF, 21-DOWN = ONE, 25-ACROSS = TIE and 32-ACROSS = TAR.
