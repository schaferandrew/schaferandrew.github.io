1. **Savings Account**
Formula: $A = P(1 + r/n)^{nt}$
$P = 5,000$
$r = 0.04$
$n = 12$ (monthly)
$t = 3$
Calculation: $5,000(1 + 0.04/12)^{36}$
$5,000(1.003333...)^{36} \approx 5,636.359$
Rounded to the cent: **$5,636.36**

2. **Flooring**
Base area: $1,850$ sq ft
Waste (10%): $1,850 \times 0.10 = 185$ sq ft
Total area needed: $1,850 + 185 = 2,035$ sq ft
Boxes needed: $2,035 / 23.5 \approx 86.5957$
Since you must buy whole boxes, round up: **87 boxes**

3. **Road Trip Gas**
Gallons used: $412 \text{ miles} / 34 \text{ mpg} \approx 12.1176$ gallons
Total cost: $12.1176 \times \$3.89 \approx 47.1376$
Rounded to the cent: **$47.14**

4. **Dinner Split**
Pre-tax amount: $\$172.00$
Tip (18% of pre-tax): $172 \times 0.18 = \$30.96$
Total bill: $\$186.40 \text{ (tax included)} + \$30.96 = \$217.36$
Split four ways: $217.36 / 4 = \$54.34$
Each person pays: **$54.34**

ANSWERS
1. $5,636.36
2. 87 boxes
3. $47.14
4. $54.34
