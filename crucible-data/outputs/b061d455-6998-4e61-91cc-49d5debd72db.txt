For a character who is highly intelligent and ambitious but emotionally guarded, I avoided "soft" or overly melodic names (like Isabella or Sophia) in favor of names with sharper consonants and cleaner lines. Since she was born around 1990, I selected names that were popular in the late 80s and early 90s but feel timeless enough for literary fiction. For the surnames, I chose common Polish-American names that are recognizable but not caricatured, balancing those with the classic "-ski" ending and shorter, punchier options like Nowak or Mazur to reflect her working-class roots.
