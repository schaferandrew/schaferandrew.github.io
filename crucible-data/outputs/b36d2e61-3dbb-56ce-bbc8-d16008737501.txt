**Calculations**

1. **Flour amount for 10 people**

   The recipe uses \(300\text{ g}\) of flour for \(4\) people.

   \[
   \frac{300\text{ g}}{4} = 75\text{ g per person}
   \]

   For \(10\) people:

   \[
   75\text{ g/person} \times 10 = 750\text{ g}
   \]

2. **Sale price of the jacket**

   Original price: \$84.60  
   Discount: \(15\%\)

   Discount amount:

   \[
   0.15 \times 84.60 = 12.69
   \]

   Sale price:

   \[
   84.60 - 12.69 = 71.91
   \]

---

**ANSWERS**

1. 750 g  
2. $71.91
