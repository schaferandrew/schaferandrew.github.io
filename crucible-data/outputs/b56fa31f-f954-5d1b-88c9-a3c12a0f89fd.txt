Created a CLI compound interest calculator with all required features:

**Files created:**
- `compound_interest.py` - Main CLI tool
- `test_compound_interest.py` - 18 unit tests (all passing)
- `README.md` - Documentation with command examples

**Features:**
- Supports years (`5y`) and months (`120m`) for time input
- Monthly compounding (`-rf 12`) with recurring contributions
- Validates negative/invalid inputs
- Prints summary: Principal, Total Contributions, Interest Earned, Final Balance
- Zero interest handling
- Known expected-value case: $1,000 at 5% for 10 years = $1,628.89
