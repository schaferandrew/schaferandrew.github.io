1. Which country is crossed by both the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn?
The obvious answer is Ecuador, but the correct answer is Brazil, which crosses both lines and extends into the Southern Hemisphere where the Tropic of Capricorn lies.

2. Which country has coastline on both the Red Sea and the Persian Gulf?
The obvious answer is Egypt, but the correct answer is Saudi Arabia, which has coastlines on both bodies of water while Egypt's Red Sea coast is in the Sinai Peninsula.

3. What is the northernmost capital city of a sovereign state?
The obvious answer is Oslo, but the correct answer is Reykjavik, as Iceland's capital lies at a higher latitude than Norway's capital.

4. Counting overseas territories, which country spans the most time zones?
The obvious answer is Russia, but the correct answer is France, which spans 12 time zones including its overseas territories in the Americas, Africa, and Pacific islands.

5. Which US state is closest to mainland Africa?
The obvious answer is Florida, but the correct answer is Maine, as it extends farther north and east than Florida, making its northern tip closest to the African coast.

6. Apart from the microstates inside Italy, which is the only landlocked country that borders exactly one other country?
The obvious answer is Switzerland, but the correct answer is Lesotho, which is completely surrounded by South Africa and has no other borders.

ANSWERS
1. Brazil
2. Saudi Arabia
3. Reykjavik
4. France
5. Maine
6. Lesotho
