### 1. Savings Account Growth
**Problem:** Calculate the future value of \$5,000 at 4% annual interest compounded monthly over 3 years.

*   **Formula:** $A = P(1 + \frac{r}{n})^{nt}$
    *   $P$ (Principal) = 5,000
    *   $r$ (Annual rate) = 0.04
    *   $n$ (Compounding periods per year) = 12
    *   $t$ (Time in years) = 3

*   **Step 1:** Calculate the monthly interest rate ($i$).
    $$i = \frac{0.04}{12} = 0.0033333333...$$

*   **Step 2:** Calculate the total number of compounding periods ($N$).
    $$N = 12 \times 3 = 36$$

*   **Step 3:** Calculate the growth factor $(1 + i)^N$.
    $$(1 + 0.0033333333)^{36} \approx 1.127159776$$

*   **Step 4:** Multiply by the principal.
    $$5,000 \times 1.127159776 = 5,635.79888...$$

*   **Step 5:** Round to the nearest cent.
    $$5,635.79888... \rightarrow 5,635.80$$

**Answer:** \$5,635.80

### 2. Flooring Boxes Calculation
**Problem:** Determine the number of 23.5 sq ft boxes needed for 1,850 sq ft plus 10% waste.

*   **Step 1:** Calculate the total area required including waste.
    $$\text{Total Area} = 1,850 \times (1 + 0.10) = 1,850 \times 1.10 = 2,035 \text{ sq ft}$$

*   **Step 2:** Divide the total area by the area per box to find the exact number of boxes needed.
    $$\text{Exact Boxes} = \frac{2,035}{23.5} \approx 86.59574468...$$

*   **Step 3:** Round up to the next whole number (since you cannot buy partial boxes).
    Since 86.59... is greater than 86, you must buy 87 boxes to cover the area.

**Answer:** 87 boxes

***

### 3. Road Trip Gas Cost
**Problem:** Calculate the cost of gas for a 412-mile trip with 34 mpg efficiency at \$3.89/gallon.

*   **Step 1:** Calculate the gallons of gas needed.
    $$\text{Gallons} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{MPG}} = \frac{412}{34} \approx 12.11764706 \text{ gallons}$$

*   **Step 2:** Calculate the total cost.
    $$\text{Cost} = \text{Gallons} \times \text{Price per Gallon}$$
    $$\text{Cost} = 12.11764706 \times 3.89 \approx 47.13764706...$$

*   **Step 3:** Round to the nearest cent.
    $$47.13764706... \rightarrow 47.14$$

**Answer:** \$47.14

***

### 4. Dinner Tip Calculation
**Problem:** Calculate the share per person for a meal where the pre-tax amount is \$172.00, tax is included in the \$186.40 total, tip is 18% of the pre-tax amount, and there are 4 people.

*   **Step 1:** Calculate the tip amount based on the pre-tax amount.
    $$\text{Tip} = 172.00 \times 0.18 = 30.96$$

*   **Step 2:** Calculate the total bill (Pre-tax + Tax + Tip).
    *   Pre-tax: 172.00
    *   Tax: $186.40 - 172.00 = 14.40$
    *   Tip: 30.96
    $$\text{Total} = 172.00 + 14.40 + 30.96 = 217.36$$

*   **Step 3:** Divide the total by the number of people (4).
    $$\text{Per Person} = \frac{217.36}{4} = 54.34$$

*   **Step 4:** Round to the nearest cent.
    $$54.34 \rightarrow 54.34$$

**Answer:** \$54.34
