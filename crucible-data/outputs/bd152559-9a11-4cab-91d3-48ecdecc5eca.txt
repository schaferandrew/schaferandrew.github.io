

1. Savings compounded monthly:  
   \[
   5000\left(1+\frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{36}
   =5000(1.003333\ldots)^{36}
   =5636.359\ldots
   \]
   Rounded to the cent: **$5,636.36**

2. Flooring with 10% extra:  
   \[
   1850 \times 1.10 = 2035 \text{ sq ft}
   \]
   \[
   2035 \div 23.5 = 86.595\ldots
   \]
   Since you must buy whole boxes: **87 boxes**

3. Gas cost:  
   \[
   412 \div 34 = 12.117647\ldots \text{ gallons}
   \]
   \[
   12.117647\ldots \times 3.89 = 47.137647\ldots
   \]
   Rounded to the cent: **$47.14**

4. Tip and split:  
   \[
   172.00 \times 0.18 = 30.96
   \]
   \[
   186.40 + 30.96 = 217.36
   \]
   \[
   217.36 \div 4 = 54.34
   \]
   Each person pays: **$54.34**

ANSWERS  
1. $5,636.36  
2. 87 boxes  
3. $47.14  
4. $54.34
