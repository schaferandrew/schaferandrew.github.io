**Reasoning**

A woman who is 34 now was born around 1990-91, so the first names should be ones a working-class Midwestern family would plausibly have chosen then: Jennifer, Megan, Lauren, Erin, Katie, and so on, with a few that were slightly less common. Polish heritage is best carried by the surname, which is realistic and common (Wojcik, Mazur, Nowak) rather than a showy "-ski" joke. I avoided heavily marked first names like Agnieszka or Bogusia, and anything that reads as a pierogi-and-polka cliché. A few first names hint at a Catholic parish upbringing or a grandmother's influence without announcing it. Since she is guarded, dry, and ambitious, I leaned toward names that are compact, a little hard-edged, or deceptively plain.
