The paragraph is short and mostly clean. Two clear errors stand out: "louder **then**" should be "louder **than**" (comparative), and "talking **to** fast" should be "talking **too** fast." Both are standard usage mistakes, not stylistic choices.

Beyond that, the punctuation is mostly deliberate-sounding: the em dash after "Still," the sentence fragment "Not waiting for her, exactly," and the comma in "laugh, or leave" all shape the rhythm. The comma before "or leave" is technically nonstandard (no comma needed with a two-item "whether... or" construction), but it creates a pause that fits the voice, so I'll flag it rather than change it. The final "And" opening a sentence is also informal but clearly a voice choice.
