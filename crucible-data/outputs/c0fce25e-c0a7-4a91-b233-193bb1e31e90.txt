## Solutions

**1. Divisible by 3 or 7, but not 21 (1 to 10,000)**

- Multiples of 3: ⌊10000/3⌋ = 3333
- Multiples of 7: ⌊10000/7⌋ = 1428
- Multiples of 21: ⌊10000/21⌋ = 476

Divisible by 3 or 7: 3333 + 1428 − 476 = 4285. Excluding multiples of 21: 4285 − 476 = **3809**.

**2. 7^2026 mod 100**

Powers of 7 mod 100 cycle with period 4: 7, 49, 43, 01. Since 2026 ≡ 2 (mod 4), the answer is **49**. (Check: 2026 mod 4 = 2, and 7² = 49 ✓)

**3. Tiling a 2×12 board**

Let f(n) be the number of tilings of a 2×n board. Look at the last column(s): a vertical domino (f(n−1) ways), or fill the last two columns completely with two horizontals, a 2×2 square, or two verticals (3·f(n−2) ways). So f(n) = f(n−1) + 3f(n−2), f(0)=1, f(1)=1:

1, 1, 4, 7, 19, 40, 97, 217, 508, 1159, 2683, 6160, **14209**

**4. Largest die exactly 4**

All three dice show values in {1,2,3,4}, minus the cases where all are in {1,2,3}:
(4³ − 3³)/6³ = (64 − 27)/216 = 37/216.

**5. Tuesday boy problem**

Count (gender, day) pairs for two children: 14² = 196 equally likely outcomes. "At least one boy born on Tuesday": 14 + 14 − 1 = 27 outcomes. "Both boys with at least one born Tuesday": boy-Tue + boy-any-day gives 7, plus boy-any-day + boy-Tue gives 7, minus double count = 13. Probability = 13/27. (The tempting wrong answer 1/3 fails because "at least one boy Tuesday" is not the same as "at least one boy.")

## ANSWERS

1. 3809
2. 49
3. 14209
4. 37/216
5. 13/27
