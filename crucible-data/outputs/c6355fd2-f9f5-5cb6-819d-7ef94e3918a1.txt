## Summary - Editorial Review Feature Complete ✅

I have successfully implemented the editorial review workflow for the RealWorld API. Here's what was accomplished:

### Core Implementation

1. **Article Status Tracking** - Added `status` ("draft", "in_review", "published") and `reviewComment` fields to all article JSON responses

2. **New Endpoints**:
   - `POST /api/articles/:slug/submit` - Authors submit drafts for review
   - `GET /api/articles/review-queue` - Editors view pending reviews (oldest first)
   - `POST /api/articles/:slug/approve` - Editors approve articles for publication
   - `POST /api/articles/:slug/reject` - Editors reject with feedback
   - `GET /api/user/articles` - Authors see their submission status

3. **Editor Configuration** - Editors configured via `EDITORS` environment variable (e.g., `export EDITORS="ada,charles,grace"`)

### All Business Rules Implemented
- ✅ Public cannot see unapproved work
- ✅ No self-approval allowed  
- ✅ Feedback is private to author and editors
- ✅ Authors can track submission status
