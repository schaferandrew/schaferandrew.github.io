

I’m aiming for names that sound native to the American Midwest while still carrying Polish family history without marking the character as exotic. The first names lean Catholic, unglamorous, and slightly old-fashioned or 1990s-practical; the surnames are real but naturalized, with enough clipped sound to match dryness, ambition, and guardedness.

2. Helen Dąbrowski — quiet and sturdy; old-fashioned dignity that can hide feeling.
3. Catherine Kowalczyk — intellectual and a little severe; dry, controlled sound.
4. Teresa Kamiński — direct and unornamented; strong enough for ambition, plain enough for working-class life.
5. Victoria Nowak — clean and capable; implies self-made polish without pretension.
