a) **Could have known.** The Emancipation Proclamation took effect on January 1, 1863, nearly six months earlier.

b) **Could have known.** The Union siege of Vicksburg had begun in May and was still under way.

c) **Could not have known.** The Battle of Gettysburg—and Lee’s defeat there—would not occur until July 1–3, 1863.

d) **Could have known.** Telegraph lines were in use, including for news from Washington.

e) **Could have known.** The *Monitor* and *Merrimack* (the *Virginia*) fought in March 1862.

f) **Could not have done this.** The telephone was not developed for practical use until the 1870s.

g) **Could not have done this with barbed wire.** Barbed-wire fence designs were patented starting in 1867, after this date.

h) **Could have known.** Brady’s photographs of Antietam’s dead were exhibited and publicized in 1862.

i) **Could have known.** Stonewall Jackson died on May 10, 1863.

j) **Could not have described it this way.** West Virginia was admitted to the Union on June 20, 1863—not the previous month, or even by June 10.

k) **Could not have known.** The New York draft riots took place in July 1863.
