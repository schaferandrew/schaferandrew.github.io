1. **Savings Account**
Formula: $A = P(1 + r/n)^{nt}$
$P = 5,000$
$r = 0.04$
$n = 12$ (monthly)
$t = 3$
$A = 5,000(1 + 0.04/12)^{12 \times 3}$
$A = 5,000(1 + 0.0033333333)^{36}$
$A = 5,000(1.0033333333)^{36}$
$A = 5,000(1.127271884)$
$A = 5,636.35942$
Rounded: $5,636.36
