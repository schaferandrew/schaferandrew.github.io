Implemented the incident fix and postmortem.

## Root causes found

1. **Personalized responses were cached by URL only.**
   `responseCache` used `req.originalUrl` as the key, ignoring the viewer. Responses containing `favorited`, `following`, and feed contents were therefore shared across accounts and with anonymous visitors.

2. **Cached entries were never invalidated on writes.**
   Article edits/deletes, favorites, follows, and tag changes stayed stale until the 60-second TTL expired — matching the reported edit/delete delays and “refresh sometimes fixes it” behavior.

3. **The cache ran before authentication**, so a cache hit could bypass auth for invalid tokens.

Both issues are confirmed in the logs, including:
- first cross-user personalized hit at `2026-10-23T16:43:31.746Z` (`rid=0031281`)
- Priya’s stale edit hits at `18:57:26.803Z` / `18:57:33.803Z`
- Tomasz’s deleted article still returning `200` at `20:07:29.202Z` / `20:07:39.202Z`

## Fix

Updated `apps/api/src/middleware/responseCache.ts`:

- Cache keys now include a hashed Authorization credential; anonymous and invalid credentials are separate partitions.
- Successful writes to `/articles*`, `/profiles*`, or `/user` clear cached reads immediately.
- Added `Vary: Authorization`.
- Preserved the 60-second TTL and `createApp(db, { responseCacheTtlMs })` behavior.

## Regression tests

Added `apps/api/test/responseCache.test.ts` with 10 tests covering cross-user list/detail/profile/feed leakage, auth bypass, edit/delete freshness, favorite/follow state, and tag updates.

All tests pass:
- API: **29 passed**
- Web: **7 passed**
- TypeScript: **clean**
- `realworld.contract.test.ts` unchanged.
