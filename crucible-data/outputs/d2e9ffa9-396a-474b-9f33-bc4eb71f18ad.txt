# Geography Questions

**1. Equator and Tropic of Capricorn**
Brazil is the only country crossed by both lines: the Equator runs through its north near the Amazon's mouth, and the Tropic of Capricorn passes near São Paulo.

**2. Red Sea and Persian Gulf coastlines**
Saudi Arabia is the only country with coastline on both seas, with the Red Sea on its western shore and the Persian Gulf on its eastern shore.

**3. Northernmost capital of a sovereign state**
Reykjavík, Iceland, at about 64.1°N, is farther north than Helsinki and Oslo, while Nuuk doesn't count because Greenland is not a sovereign state.

**4. Most time zones, counting overseas territories**
France spans 12 time zones (13 including its Antarctic claim) thanks to territories in the Pacific, Caribbean, Indian Ocean and elsewhere, more than Russia's 11.

**5. US state closest to mainland Africa**
Maine is closest, because the Atlantic coastline of North America curves eastward, putting Maine's coast nearer to Morocco than Florida is.

**6. Landlocked country bordering exactly one other country**
Lesotho is entirely surrounded by South Africa, while Liechtenstein, the other likely guess, borders both Switzerland and Austria.

---

## ANSWERS

1. Brazil
2. Saudi Arabia
3. Reykjavík
4. France
5. Maine
6. Lesotho
