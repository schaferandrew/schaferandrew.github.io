## What I built

**Package `interest/`**
- `interest/core.py` — `calculate()` models interest credited at the end of each compounding period (`annual_rate / periods_per_year` on the balance at that moment), with contributions added at the end of their month/year interval *after* same-moment interest. Handles 1/4/12/365 periods per year, partial-period durations (e.g. 6 months compounded annually), and rounds results to cents.
- `interest/cli.py` — argparse-based CLI with validation and readable/JSON output.
- `interest/__main__.py` — enables `python -m interest`.

**Flags** — all required/optional flags implemented, including the mutually exclusive `--years`/`--months`, paired `--contribution`/`--contribution-frequency`, and `--json`.

**Errors** — invalid/negative/contradictory input exits non-zero with a short stderr message (argparse or `parser.error`), never a traceback.

**Tests `tests/test_interest.py`** — 11 tests covering the known 5% 10-year annual case (1628.89), zero interest, monthly compounding, contributions at both frequencies, annual compounding + monthly contributions, partial-year duration, JSON shape, and 10 invalid-input cases.

**README.md** — requirements, run instructions, options table, examples, error behavior, and test commands.
