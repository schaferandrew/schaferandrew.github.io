The events span from the medieval period into the late 20th century. To arrange them chronologically, I have assigned each event to its approximate year and then ordered them from the earliest date (Magna Carta) to the latest date (Fall of the Berlin Wall).

ORDER:
1. The signing of Magna Carta — 1215
2. The printing of the Gutenberg Bible — c. 1450
3. The start of the French Revolution — 1789
4. The start of the First World War — 1914
5. The first Moon landing — 1969
6. The fall of the Berlin Wall — 1989
