The obvious answer for the first question is often mistaken for Brazil or Indonesia, but those nations are only crossed by the Equator, whereas Brazil is the only country crossed by both the Equator and the Tropic of Capricorn.

While Iran borders the Persian Gulf, it does not touch the Red Sea, making Saudi Arabia the correct answer as it is the only nation with coastlines on both bodies of water.

Reykjavik is frequently cited as the northernmost capital, but Nuuk in Greenland (part of the Kingdom of Denmark) is geographically further north, making it the northernmost capital of a sovereign state.

France is often assumed to have the most time zones due to its overseas territories, but Russia actually spans 11 time zones, which is more than France's 12 if you count all overseas regions, though typically Russia is cited as having 11 and France 12; however, if counting strictly sovereign states without overseas territories, Russia has 11, while France has 12 including overseas. Let's re-evaluate: France has 12 time zones due to overseas territories. Russia has 11. So France is the correct answer. The "obvious" answer might be Russia due to its size, but France's overseas possessions give it the edge.

Mainland Africa is closest to the US state of Maine, not Florida or California, because the easternmost point of Africa (Ras Hafun) is closer to the northeastern tip of the US than any other US state.

Vatican City and San Marino are microstates inside Italy, but the question asks for the only *other* landlocked country bordering exactly one other country. This is a trick question because there are no other such countries; however, if we consider the definition of "country" strictly, there are none. Wait, let's look at the list of landlocked countries. Most border multiple. The only ones bordering one country are Vatican City, San Marino, and... actually, there are no others. But the question says "Apart from the microstates inside Italy". This implies there is one. Let's check again. Lesotho borders only South Africa. Eswatini borders only South Africa? No, Eswatini borders Mozambique and South Africa. Lesotho is entirely surrounded by South Africa. So Lesotho is the answer. The obvious answer might be Vatican City, but it is excluded.
