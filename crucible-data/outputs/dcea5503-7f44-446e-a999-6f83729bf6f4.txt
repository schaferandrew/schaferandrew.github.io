5. **Storming of the Bastille, July 1789** – Correct (14 July 1789).

7. **Titanic sank in April 1913** – Wrong year. She sank on 15 April **1912**.
8. **India independent, August 1947** – Correct (15 August 1947).
9. **Armstrong & Aldrin on the Moon, July 1969** – Correct (20 July 1969).
10. **Berlin Wall fell, November 1991** – Wrong year. It fell on 9 November **1989**.
11. **Germany reunified the year after the Wall fell** – Correct. Reunification was 3 October 1990, the year after 1989.
