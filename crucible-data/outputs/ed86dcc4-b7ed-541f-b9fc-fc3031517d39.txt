**Question 1**
To find the future value of the savings account, we use the compound interest formula:
$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$$

Where:
*   $P$ (Principal) = $\$5,000$
*   $r$ (Annual Interest Rate) = $4\% = 0.04$
*   $n$ (Compounding Frequency) = $12$ (monthly)
*   $t$ (Time in years) = $3$

**Working:**
1.  Calculate the monthly interest rate: $\frac{0.04}{12} \approx 0.0033333333$
2.  Add 1 to the monthly rate: $1 + 0.0033333333 = 1.0033333333$
3.  Calculate the total number of compounding periods: $12 \times 3 = 36$
4.  Raise the monthly rate factor to the power of 36: $(1.0033333333)^{36} \approx 1.12727185$
5.  Multiply by the principal: $5,000 \times 1.12727185 \approx 5,636.35925$

**Rounding:**
Round to the nearest cent: **$\$5,636.36$**

**Question 3**

**Working:**

**Question 4**
Calculate the tip based on the pre-tax amount, add it to the total bill (including tax), and divide by four.

**Working:**

2.  Calculate total cost to split: $\$186.40 \text{ (bill)} + \$30.96 \text{ (tip)} = \$217.36$

The result is exact to the cent: **$\$54.34$**
