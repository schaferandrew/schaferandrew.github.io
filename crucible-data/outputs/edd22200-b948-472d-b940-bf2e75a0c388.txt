**Approach**

I chose names that feel contemporary, unpretentious, and regionally plausible for someone from the upper Midwest while avoiding overtly Polish‑American or ethnic markers. The focus was on first names that are common in American literary circles (e.g., “Mara,” “Juno”) paired with surnames that could plausibly arise from a working‑class background—often simple, one‑syllable or two‑syllable last names that don’t carry strong ethnic connotations. Each name is followed by a brief note explaining why it suits the character’s intelligence, dry humor, ambition, and guarded emotional nature.
