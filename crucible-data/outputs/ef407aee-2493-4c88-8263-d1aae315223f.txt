# Everyday Money & Measurement

## Problem 1
Compound interest: $A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$

$$A = 5000\left(1 + \frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{12\times 3} = 5000(1.0033333\ldots)^{36}$$

$$(1.0033333\ldots)^{36} \approx 1.12727187$$

$$A = 5000 \times 1.12727187 = 5636.3594$$

**$5,636.36**

## Problem 2
Account for waste first:
$$1850 \times 1.10 = 2035 \text{ sq ft}$$

Boxes needed:
$$\frac{2035}{23.5} = 86.60$$

Since you can't buy a partial box, round **up**:

**87 boxes**

## Problem 3
Gallons used × price per gallon (keep full precision until the end):
$$\frac{412}{34} \times 3.89 = \frac{1602.68}{34} = 47.13765$$

**$47.14**

## Problem 4
Tip on pre-tax amount:
$$0.18 \times 172.00 = 30.96$$

Total (bill with tax + tip):
$$186.40 + 30.96 = 217.36$$

Split four ways:
$$\frac{217.36}{4} = 54.34$$

**$54.34 each**

## ANSWERS
1. $5,636.36
2. 87 boxes
3. $47.14
4. $54.34
