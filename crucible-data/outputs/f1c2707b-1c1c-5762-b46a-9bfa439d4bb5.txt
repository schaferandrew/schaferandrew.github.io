**1. Savings Account Value**

This is a compound interest problem using the formula: $A = P(1 + r/n)^{(nt)}$

*   $P$ (Principal) = $\$5,000$
*   $r$ (Annual rate) = $4\% = 0.04$
*   $n$ (Compounding periods per year) = $12$ (monthly)
*   $t$ (Time in years) = $3$

**Working:**
1. Calculate the total number of compounding periods ($nt$): $3 \times 12 = 36$
2. Calculate the monthly interest rate ($r/n$): $0.04 / 12$
3. Substitute into the formula:
$$A = 5000 \left(1 + \frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{36}$$
$$A = 5000 (1 + 0.003333...)^{36}$$
$$A = 5000 (1.003333...)^{36}$$
$$A = 5000 \times 1.13309845...$$
$$A = 5665.4922...$$

Rounding to the cent: $\$5,665.49$

**2. Flooring Boxes Needed**

First, calculate the total area needed including waste. Then, determine how many boxes cover that area.

*   Required area = $1,850$ sq ft
*   Waste allowance = $10\%$

**Working:**
1. Calculate the total area needed (including waste):
$$\text{Total Area} = 1,850 \times (1 + 0.10)$$
$$\text{Total Area} = 1,850 \times 1.10$$
$$\text{Total Area} = 2,035 \text{ sq ft}$$

2. Calculate the number of boxes:
$$\text{Number of Boxes} = \frac{\text{Total Area}}{\text{Box Size}}$$
$$\text{Number of Boxes} = \frac{2,035}{23.5}$$
$$\text{Number of Boxes} = 87$$

You need exactly 87 boxes.

**3. Gas Cost Calculation**

First, determine the total gallons needed for the trip. Then, calculate the cost based on the price per gallon.

*   Distance = $412$ miles
*   Efficiency = $34$ miles/gallon
*   Price = $\$3.89$/gallon

**Working:**
1. Calculate the total gallons required:
$$\text{Gallons} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{MPG}}$$
$$\text{Gallons} = \frac{412}{34}$$
$$\text{Gallons} \approx 12.117647...$$

2. Calculate the total cost:
$$\text{Cost} = \text{Gallons} \times \text{Price per gallon}$$
$$\text{Cost} = \frac{412}{34} \times 3.89$$
$$\text{Cost} = 12.117647... \times 3.89$$
$$\text{Cost} = 47.1265...$$

Rounding to the cent: $\$47.13$

**4. Tipping and Splitting Bill**

First, calculate the tip amount based on the pre-tax total. Then, find the grand total and divide it equally among four people.

*   Pre-tax amount = $\$172.00$
*   Tip percentage = $18\%$

**Working:**
1. Calculate the tip amount:
$$\text{Tip} = 172.00 \times 0.18$$
$$\text{Tip} = \$30.96$$

2. Calculate the grand total:
$$\text{Grand Total} = \text{Pre-tax amount} + \text{Tip}$$
$$\text{Grand Total} = 172.00 + 30.96$$
$$\text{Grand Total} = \$202.96$$

3. Calculate each person's share:
$$\text{Share per Person} = \frac{\text{Grand Total}}{4}$$
$$\text{Share per Person} = \frac{202.96}{4}$$
$$\text{Share per Person} = \$50.74$$
