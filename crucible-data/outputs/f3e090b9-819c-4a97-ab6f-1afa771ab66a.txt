**1. Savings Account**
To calculate compound interest, we use the formula: $A = P(1 + \frac{r}{n})^{nt}$
*   Principal ($P$) = $5,000
*   Annual interest rate ($r$) = 0.04
*   Compounding periods per year ($n$) = 12
*   Years ($t$) = 3

Calculation:
$A = 5000(1 + \frac{0.04}{12})^{12 \times 3}$
$A = 5000(1 + 0.00333333333)^{36}$
$A = 5000(1.00333333333)^{36}$
$A = 5000(1.12727188)$
$A = 5636.3594...$

Rounded to the cent: **$5,636.36**

***

**2. Flooring Boxes**
*   Base area = 1,850 sq ft
*   Waste factor = 1.10 (10% extra)
*   Box coverage = 23.5 sq ft

Calculation:
Total area needed = $1850 \times 1.10 = 2035$ sq ft
Number of boxes = $2035 / 23.5 = 86.5957...$

Since you cannot buy a partial box, you must round up to the nearest whole number.
Total boxes: **87**

***

**3. Road Trip Gas Cost**
*   Distance = 412 miles
*   Fuel efficiency = 34 mpg
*   Gas price = $3.89 per gallon

Calculation:
Gallons needed = $412 / 34 = 12.117647...$
Total cost = $12.117647... \times 3.89 = 47.137647...$

Rounded to the cent: **$47.14**

***

**4. Dinner Split**
*   Total bill (including tax) = $186.40
*   Pre-tax amount = $172.00
*   Tip rate = 18% (on pre-tax)
*   Number of people = 4

Calculation:
Tip amount = $172.00 \times 0.18 = 30.96$
Total amount to pay = $186.40 + 30.96 = 217.36$
Split per person = $217.36 / 4 = 54.34$

Each person pays: **$54.34**
