## Analysis of the Clues

**Given:**
- 4 chairs (1, 2, 3, 4) from left to right
- 4 friends: Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave
- 4 colors: red, blue, green, yellow (each person has a different color)

**Clue Summary:**
1. Alice sits immediately left of the person who likes blue
2. Bob does not sit in chair 4
3. Green is in chair 1
4. Carol likes yellow and sits right of the person who likes red
5. Dave likes red

## Step-by-Step Solution

**From clue 3:** Chair 1 = green (favorite color)

**From clues 4 & 5:** 
- Carol's favorite color = yellow
- Dave's favorite color = red

So the remaining colors are green and blue, which must belong to Alice or Bob.

**From clue 1:** "Alice sits immediately to the left of the person who likes blue"

This means:
- Alice does NOT like blue (she's to the left of someone else who does)
- Therefore, **Bob must like blue**, and **Alice must like green**

**Now we know:**
- Chair 1: Green → Must be Alice (since Alice likes green)
- Bob likes blue
- Dave likes red
- Carol likes yellow

**Placing people in chairs:**

**From clues 4 & 5:** Carol (yellow) sits right of the person who likes red (Dave).
- Remaining chairs: 3 and 4
- Carol must be to the right of Dave
- Therefore: Dave in chair 3, Carol in chair 4
