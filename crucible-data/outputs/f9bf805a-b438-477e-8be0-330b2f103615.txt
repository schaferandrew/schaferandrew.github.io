1. Brazil is crossed by both, since the Equator passes through the north and the Tropic of Capricorn through the south.  
2. Saudi Arabia has coasts on both the Red Sea and the Persian Gulf (though Egypt also touches the Red Sea and Gulf of Aqaba, not the Persian Gulf).  
3. Longyearbyen (Svalbard) is not a capital, so the correct answer is Reykjavík, Iceland — but the obvious “northernmost” trick answer is Nuuk (Greenland), which is actually farther south than Reykjavík.  
4. France, counting its overseas territories (e.g., French Polynesia, Guadeloupe, Réunion), spans 12 time zones, more than the US’s 11.  
5. Maine is the US state closest to mainland Africa, because its easternmost point (West Quoddy Head) is closer than Florida to the African coast.  
6. Lesotho is the only landlocked country (besides microstates) that borders exactly one country, South Africa.  

ANSWERS  
1. Brazil  
2. Saudi Arabia  
3. Reykjavík (Iceland)  
4. France  
5. Maine  
6. Lesotho
