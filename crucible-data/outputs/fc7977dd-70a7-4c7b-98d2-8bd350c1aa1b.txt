**1. Savings account balance**

Use the compound-interest formula  
\(A=P\left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}\)

- \(P = 5{,}000\)
- \(r = 0.04\)
- \(n = 12\)
- \(t = 3\)

\[
A = 5{,}000\left(1+\frac{0.04}{12}\right)^{12\times3}
   = 5{,}000(1.003333\ldots)^{36}
   = 5{,}000 \times 1.127271872\ldots
   = 5{,}636.35936\ldots
\]

Rounded to the nearest cent: **\$5,636.36**

---

**2. Flooring boxes needed**

Total area including 10% waste:

\[
1{,}850 \times 1.10 = 2{,}035 \text{ sq ft}
\]

Boxes required:

\[
\frac{2{,}035}{23.5} = 86.5957\ldots
\]

Since partial boxes cannot be purchased, round up to the next whole number: **87 boxes**  
(86 boxes cover only \(86\times23.5=2{,}021\) sq ft, which is not enough.)

---

**3. Cost of gas for the trip**

Gallons needed:

\[
\frac{412}{34} = 12.117647\ldots \text{ gal}
\]

Total cost:

\[
12.117647\ldots \times 3.89 = 47.137647\ldots
\]

Rounded to the nearest cent: **\$47.14**

---

**4. Splitting the dinner bill**

Tip (18% on pre-tax amount):

\[
172.00 \times 0.18 = 30.96
\]

Total to be split:

\[
186.40 + 30.96 = 217.36
\]

Each person’s share:

\[
\frac{217.36}{4} = 54.34
\]

**\$54.34** per person.
