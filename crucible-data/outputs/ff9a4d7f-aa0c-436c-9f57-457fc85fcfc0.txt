Here's my reasoning for ordering these events:

- **Magna Carta** was signed in 1215 at Runnymede, England.
- **The Gutenberg Bible** was printed around 1455 in Mainz, Germany.
- **The French Revolution** began in 1789 (Storming of the Bastille, July 14).
- **The First World War** began in 1914 (July/August).
- **The first Moon landing** (Apollo 11) occurred in 1969 (July 20).
- **The fall of the Berlin Wall** happened in 1989 (November 9).

So the chronological order runs from the 13th century through the 20th century.
