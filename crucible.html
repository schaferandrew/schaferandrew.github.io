Crucible

Under construction. Crucible is a work in progress: tests, scoring and results are still changing, and some numbers here come from early or partial runs. Check back soon.

Crucible is an AI task gauntlet to analyze whether local models are capable of real work. Can they write code for personal projects? Keep working when the internet is unavailable? Accomplish tasks involving personal data we don’t want to share with cloud providers for training? The tasks are divided into chat and agentic sections and are categorized into three tiers of difficulty. Play around, I think you’ll be surprised by the performance of these small models.

Everything below was created and written by or with the help of AI.
